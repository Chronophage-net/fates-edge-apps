Appendix B: Optional Rules and Tactical Tools

Fate's Edge GM Guide — Appendix B

Grid-Based Combat (Optional)

Use a square or hex grid with Zones of Control (ZoC).

ZoC rules:

  • Entering an enemy's Zone of Control ends your movement — you are engaged.
  • You cannot move through an enemy's ZoC.
  • Leaving a ZoC requires the Disengage action (DV 4) or spending 1 Boon.
  • Flank: +1 die to attack. Rear: +1 die and +1 Effect.

Push-Through: Roll Body+Athletics vs. DV 4. Choose a cost on success: Position worsens by one step, −1 die on the action, or the enemy gets a free opportunity attack. A tougher enemy does not raise the DV — it worsens your Position, as everywhere else in the game.

Detailed Warfare (Mass Combat)

Treat an army as a Follower with a high Capability and its own timers. It assists at min(Cap, the relevant Skill) like any other Follower, and no roll takes more than +3 dice from all sources combined.

  • Supply Timer [6] or [8]: starts full and empties through use. When empty, the army suffers severe penalties — every roll it assists is one Position step worse.
  • Morale Timer [6] or [8]: starts empty and fills through loss. When full, the army routs.
  • Sway rolls (Presence + Sway or Wits + Insight) advance objective timers (e.g., Break Their Line [6]).
  • Battle's Edge: Story Beats generated by command rolls can be spent on fog-of-war complications — false orders, broken supply wagons, or a messenger's death.

Worked example. The Forge of Souls is built entirely on this section plus Miniatures Mode below — forces as Followers with Supply and Morale, command rolls filling objective timers, and one module-specific consequence layered on top. Read it if you want to see the warfare rules carrying a whole adventure rather than a single battle.

Hex-Based Exploration

Map a region in 6-mile hexes. Entering a hex requires a Wits + Lore roll (DV set by terrain). Outcomes determine discovery (find a point of interest), danger (ambush, hazard), or becoming lost (add a timer segment to find the way out).

Miniatures Mode — Speed Defaults

Base DV: start at DV 3 and add 1 per complicating keyword (elevation, [WARD]), to a maximum of DV 5. Disengage is DV 4. A tougher opponent does not raise the DV — it worsens your Position, which is what Position is for.

Tens: a 10 is 2 successes, as everywhere else. In miniatures mode you may spend the surplus success to Push or Pull the target 1 hex.

Zone of Control (ZoC): Entering or leaving a hex adjacent to an enemy provokes one Reaction (Free Strike or Shove 1 hex).

Heat: On a critical hit, the GM may spend 1 Heat (a resource tracked per scene) to degrade Position or trigger a terrain hazard.

Hex Keywords

Difficult: Costs 2 movement points per hex. Elevation: Attacks from lower ground suffer +1 DV. ZoC: Provokes a Reaction on crossing.

Altar [WARD]: −1 die to attacks (or attacker may spend 2 Fatigue to ignore).

Incorporeal: Ignores Difficult terrain; can pass through occupied hexes but cannot end movement in one.

Sample NPCs

Encounters (TL 2–4)

  • Bandit Skirmisher: Body 2, Wits 2. Melee 2, Stealth 1.
  • Ykrul Rider: Body 4, Wits 3. Athletics 3, Melee 3.
  • Street Bravo: Presence 3, Body 2. Melee 3.

Foils & Rivals (TL 4–6)

  • Ambitious Scribe: Wits 3, Presence 3. Deception 3, Lore 2.
  • Mercenary Captain: Body 4, Spirit 3. Sway 3, Melee 4.
  • Flame Preacher: Presence 4, Spirit 3. Performance 4, Lore 3.

Prestige NPCs (TL 7–9)

  • High Elf Loremaster: Wits 5, Spirit 4. Lore 5, Arcana 4.
  • Dwarven Forge-Patriarch: Body 5, Spirit 4. Craft 5, Sway 4.
  • Ykrul Warglord: Body 5, Presence 4. Sway 4, Melee 5.

Common Rolls (GM Screen Reference)

Athletics: climb (Body+Athletics), time a leap (Wits+Athletics)

Melee: break a guard (Body+Melee), bind a blade (Wits+Melee)

Ranged: a leading shot (Wits+Ranged), loose in a squall (Spirit+Ranged)

Unarmed: grapple (Body+Melee), feint (Wits+Melee)

Athletics: scale a wall (Body+Athletics), keep your feet on a heaving deck (Wits+Athletics)

Stealth: shadow a patrol (Wits+Stealth), hold still (Spirit+Stealth)

Endurance: resist cold (Spirit+Endurance), push through pain (Body+Endurance)

Craft: read a blueprint (Wits+Craft), brace a door (Body+Craft), field-rig a pump (Body+Craft)

Sway: haggle (Presence+Sway), rally allies (Presence+Sway), hold the line with your voice (Spirit+Sway)

Deception: lie under scrutiny (Presence+Deception), misdirect a guard (Wits+Deception)

Subterfuge: talk past a checkpoint (Presence+Deception), case a routine (Wits+Deception), find a fence (Presence+Deception)

Performance: captivate a crowd (Presence+Performance), mimic a voice (Wits+Performance)

Insight: read a room (Wits+Insight), pick the ambush lane (Wits+Insight), spot the tell (Spirit+Insight)

Lore: cite local custom (Presence+Lore), read the weather (Wits+Lore), perform a rite (Spirit+Lore)

Investigation: reconstruct a scene (Wits+Insight), interview a witness (Presence+Insight)

Medicine: stabilize a wound (Wits+Craft), cut away rot (Body+Craft)

Arcana: read a ward anchor (Wits+Arcana), hold a rite steady (Spirit+Arcana)

Fast Boundaries:

  • Locks & Traps: Mechanical = Attribute + Craft; Arcane = Attribute + Arcana.
  • People vs. Mechanisms: Subterfuge for social infiltration; Stealth to avoid detection; Mechanics or Arcana to bypass a physical or magical barrier.
  • Formal vs. Informal: Diplomacy for courts and treaties; Sway for bargaining and personal persuasion.
