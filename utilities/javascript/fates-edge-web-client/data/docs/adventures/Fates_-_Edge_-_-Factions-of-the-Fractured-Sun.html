Factions of the Fractured Sun & the Hedge-Law Realm

Political Factions of Vhasia and Viterra, 838–841 AR
Companion reference to The Fenwood-Everblood Chronicles & Within the Demon's Eye · Faction Reference
Contents
Vhasia — The Fractured Sun Viterra — The Hedge-Law Realm The Faction Triad — How They Pull

The succession crisis is not a war between armies so much as a war between ledgers. Eight factions, four to a realm, each hold a piece of the throne's legitimacy and none hold all of it. Every faction below lists its figurehead, its power base, and its aim — use these as pressure points for political-intrigue scenes, not as fixed outcomes.

Vhasia — The Fractured Sun

The Longblood Court
FigureheadValdais Longblood, Duke of Adria
Power BaseAdria, Ethor
AimProve the western line's legitimacy beyond doubt; unite Vhasia under a strong crown.

Valdais's claim rests on a single unresolved question: whether he is Wymund IV's son by birthright or Baldvyn the Good's son by blood. He has spent thirteen years being pardoned rather than proven, and that pardon is the leverage the Fenwoods still hold over him. His court plays the long game in public and the short game in private — every rumor about the Dauphin's parentage either strengthens or wrecks his own claim, so he cannot afford to let anyone else control that story.

The Queen-Mother's Household
FigureheadElianor Everblood, twice-married, twice-doubted
Power BaseEthor
AimProtect the Dauphin (real, false, or useful) until the realm chooses whose blood to believe.

Elianor is Valdais's mother and was Baldvyn the Good's second wife — which makes her household technically allied with the Longblood Court and quietly working against it. She alone may know the true answer to the Dauphin's legitimacy, and she has spent a lifetime learning that an unopened ledger is worth more than a closed one. Her household is small, aged, and dangerous precisely because no one expects an old woman's retinue to be the fulcrum of a kingdom.

The Shadow-Court Remnant
FigureheadNo acknowledged figurehead
Power BaseScattered; strongest in the Renard highlands
AimKeep faith with Myrmis in secret; treat the succession crisis as a debt the goddess is collecting.

What survived the Seven Week Uprising did not surrender — it went quiet. The Shadow-Court Remnant reads every death in the crisis (Wyllmot at Eryas, Brightstride's poisoning, whatever comes next) as a payment against an old bargain: Alaysha's wedding-eve prayer for warriors and daughters. They do not need to win the throne. They need someone with Renard blood to sit on it, and they are patient about which someone.

The Fenwood Interest
FigureheadDuke Braedyn Fenwood
Power BaseIstovar, Belworth (via Viterra)
AimConvert a war debt into a permanent seat at every Vhasian table that matters.

House Fenwood invaded and burned Lence in 832 AR, then spent every year afterward teaching the court to call the atrocity history: marriages, chancelleries, and a granddaughter on the throne itself. Braedyn never commits the same aggression twice; he arranges for its beneficiaries to defend what it built. His death in 840 AR leaves Jaennis, Ylena, and every patient heir with the same question: whether loyalty to the house requires preserving the advantage or answering the people whose loss made it possible.

Viterra — The Hedge-Law Realm

The Crown
FigureheadQueen Veralyn Everblood, “Demon-Eye”
Power BaseValora, Aberiel
AimConvert conquest into legitimacy before the earls, barons, or grief convert it back into war.

Veralyn holds Viterra because House Fenwood built her the throne and because no one currently alive can match her on a battlefield. She does not yet hold it because a court believes a killer can be a queen. Every scene at her court should test whether she is learning statecraft faster than her enemies are learning her weaknesses — Shadowsteel included.

The Northern Earls
FigureheadEarl Morcar
Power BaseUlsven
AimRestore the old pre-Fenwood balance of power; wait for the Queen's first real misstep.

The Northern Earls remember a Viterra where the crown needed their spears more than it needed a chancellor's good handwriting. They have no love for House Fenwood's audits, marriages, and seals, and they are counting on Veralyn's inexperience — or her blade's hunger — to hand them an opening.

The Eastern Barons
FigureheadNo unified figurehead
Power BaseArgona
AimReclaim the spoils they believe the Lence campaign owed them.

The barons who bled at Lence expected land, title, and a share of Vhasian tribute in exchange. What they got instead was a Fenwood chancellery that itemizes every promise and pays out on its own schedule. Their grievance is real, their patience is thin, and they are the faction most likely to ally with the Northern Earls if the Crown stumbles twice.

House Fenwood
FigureheadDuke Braedyn Fenwood, later his heirs
Power BaseBelworth, Tarlington
AimHold the throne they built, patiently, one audit and one marriage at a time.

House Fenwood is not trying to rule Viterra outright — that was never the game. It is trying to remain the only house the crown cannot function without. Every marriage (Jaennis to Brightstride), every war (Lence), every mercy (Valdais, Logan Thornhill) has been a deliberate move in a game three generations deep. Braedyn's death does not settle it. It only changes whose hand is on the pen.

The Faction Triad — How They Pull

No faction above can win alone, and that is the point. Use this triad to keep political-intrigue scenes honest:

The Blade
Myrmis's remnant faith, and whatever it is Veralyn carries — the pull toward settling a thing in blood, now, before the doubt can get a word in.
The Long Game
Kon'reh at court — the Fenwood discipline of clauses, patience, and waiting, always available, never free of its own cost.
The Question
The Dauphin's legitimacy and Valdais's parentage — the single unresolved fact every faction is racing to control or conceal.

A well-run intrigue scene lets the party feel at least two of these pulling at once. See Within the Demon's Eye, Act III: The Long Game for a full pressure-point structure built on exactly this triad.
