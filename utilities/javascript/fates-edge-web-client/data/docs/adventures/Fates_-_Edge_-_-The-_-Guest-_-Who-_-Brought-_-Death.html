A wedding in Ubral

A guest dies during a wedding feast in Hearth-Hall. The host protects everyone from immediate reprisal, including the unknown killer. Before the household can discover what happened, a sealed passage beneath its hearth begins admitting the voices of people it once refused to shelter.

This is a social mystery and household gothic adventure for an early-career party, suitable for Tiers I–II with opposition adjusted to the group. Allow two or three sessions: the feast and investigation; the threatened household and discovery of the refuge; the evacuation and opening. A single long session can begin at the death and omit the outside muster. The associated novel chooses one outcome; the adventure does not require it.

Tone: wet wool steaming near a good fire; wedding flowers beside a washed corpse; laughter audible through a wall after the room beyond has emptied. Let the hall be comfortable and desirable before it becomes dangerous. Its inhabitants have different interests, tempers and senses of humour. Avoid an endless sequence of solemn speeches about oaths.

Content to establish with the table: poisoning, coercion within families, threatened sacrifice, remembered massacre, haunting by familiar voices. No character must surrender a loved one’s memory or accept a permanent curse to complete this scenario.

The truth for the GM

  1. Odran Vale is a travelling ward-reader employed by descendants of families once denied refuge here. He is neither an Oath-Breaker assassin nor an agent of a hidden final villain. He brings evidence of Bran Dorn’s recent passage extortion and an outer threshold stone from the old refuge.

  2. Bran, the laird’s brother, poisons Odran’s individual warmed cup during a disputed wedding song. Use a fictional substance, widowglass. No real preparation, dose or recipe is needed. Its pale residue catches in the cup’s repaired seam. Bran intends to conceal the documents and blame the incoming Lorns.

  3. Lira previously helped Bran examine the sealed refuge. At his request she restored its inner threshold stone beneath the bridal chamber, believing it protective. Odran’s matching stone, carried across the present welcome, reopens the old relation. His murder supplies a fresh death under protection. Bran did not foresee the full consequence.

  4. The ancestors of House Dorn accepted displaced travellers, barred the refuge’s lower door and surrendered them to attackers. Later construction enclosed the threshold. A local binding kept the rejected dead outside by treating their admission as unfinished. It is a practice of this site, not universal Ubral law.

  5. Bran offers Lira as the person to put outside now. She was fostered among the Lorns, which makes her politically convenient to disown. Expelling her repeats the original harm; it does not safely discharge the binding.

  6. The remnant called the Keeper of the Empty Place is the refuge’s last custodian, bound to repeat the refusal. Opening and holding the original passage while returning both threshold pieces allows the rejected guests to leave. Current guests must have real shelter during the work. Exposure, a falling chimney and armed relatives are physical problems even after the haunting is understood.

The local settlement

House Dorn is a minor Mac Tern client; House Lorn is tied to Mac Gowan. The wedding addresses their own grazing and winter-passage dispute, not the enduring Mac Tern–O’Creagh feud. Varrak is the local laird, not High Chieftain of Ubral. Six neighbouring households have brought dependants, musicians, drovers and gifts. The Tor envoy witnesses arrangements but cannot absolve murder or command the dead.

Ubral is predominantly human. Druven’s Aeler family has lived here for generations and belongs to the place. Do not treat her as a visiting dispenser of magical stone answers. Her structural knowledge requires access and examination.

Guest-right at this hall

Varrak promises protection from reprisal and a hearing before punishment. That permits guarded custody, separating fighters, securing weapons and examining evidence. It does not make an assailant untouchable, forbid accusations or prevent self-defence. He offers shelter through the storm and escorted departure when possible. A guest may refuse further hospitality. A person who arrived after the death cannot truthfully be claimed as its witness.

Explain these local terms in ordinary speech. Do not spring an undisclosed hospitality rule to punish a player. The metaphysical danger belongs to the old refuge’s specific history and can be investigated separately from the laird’s current authority.

People with something to lose

A widow who wants both Massen and a life outside her father’s house. She knows household routes and routines. She initially protects its reputation and may have offended a useful witness. In play she can obtain access and admit mistakes; she does not solve the case for the party.

A capable escort captain, affectionate, proud and anxious about his people. He secretly called armed cousins to the ford as insurance. He can recall them, but a messenger must reach them and their commander may demand reassurance.

Generous in public and often in private. He will stop immediate revenge. He knows the refuge was closed after a massacre but has avoided the particulars. Later he considers Bran’s proposed sacrifice; an argument, new evidence or a workable evacuation can move him.

Familiar, useful and capable of affection. His extortion depends on control of the passage. He will hide the cup and silence its witness if allowed. Killing him does not end the haunting; saving his life does not pardon him.

Fostered among Lorns, competent with old songs and local routes. Bran used her help restoring the inner stone. She does not initially know about the poison. She later moves the corpse because he tells her it will end the dangerous welcome. Fear and exhaustion do not make all her perceptions false.

Knows who handled the cups and guards the servants’ privacy. She will give practical information. She will not surrender everyone in her care to an unannounced interrogation.

Ubral-born Aeler, with an apprentice son. Her flue repairs do not imply knowledge of the hidden foundation. Once she examines it, she can identify the danger to the chimney and propose supports. She needs labour and time, not a speech about trusting experts.

A witness with limited escort authority. Can arrange custody and carry evidence to a later hearing. Has no supernatural means of detecting lies.

Name them with the group or regional generator. Saw Bran at the warmer. Has reason to distrust Brin or the laird. Let them specify a safe place and a second listener before giving testimony.

Ground plan and opening

The old tower backs onto a timber-roofed feast hall. A service hatch connects the cup warmer to the kitchen. Brin’s place is visible from the warmer, but a standing singer blocks it during the disputed song. The women’s stair reaches the bridal chamber above a sealed lower passage. Kitchen, yard, washhouse and byre form a route that avoids the feast hall. The blocked refuge door opens towards a burn and cairn; the ford is farther downhill. Nearby cottages remain reachable before the pass does.

Describe these routes before they matter. The storm closes the distant pass first, not every door in the compound. Supernatural distortions affect the old passage and doors connected to it; they are not permission to relocate any clue the players approach.

The party may be wedding guests, paid escorts, repair workers, the envoy’s companions or travellers explicitly admitted that afternoon. Begin before the death when possible. Give them a real task and a social connection: settle a horse at the ford, help air bedding, rehearse a song, carry a relative upstairs. Odran arrives on the third afternoon of the gathering and dies that evening. There is no second unexplained “third night.”

He refuses Bran’s request to postpone his petition. During the song Bran uses the warmer. Later Odran lifts his cup, tries to speak and collapses. The red spill is wine, not a poison diagnosis. A healer tries to save him; in the default opening it is too late. If a group with appropriate powers prevents the death, honour that success: Odran can testify to Bran’s threat but did not see the dosing. The reunited stones can still disturb the refuge, with more time to evacuate and no fresh murder feeding it.

Investigation without a single locked clue

Give information when characters take an appropriate action. A roll can determine speed, notice, access under opposition or the safety of handling something. It should not decide whether the essential facts exist.

Question Direct evidence Other route or limitation
Was the drink targeted? Residue in one cup’s repaired seam; shared jug and other cups clean. The healer and cup-washer can compare them. Wine colour proves nothing.
Who had access? Serving witness saw Bran at the warmer during the song. Brin saw him approach it; a musician recalls the interrupted exchange. No witness saw every movement.
Why kill Odran? Recent extortion demand in his chest bears Bran’s damaged seal-mark. A claimant at the ford retains a matching demand. Motive alone is not proof of poisoning.
What links Bran to poison? Lira knows a stoppered packet is missing from his work chest; wrapping carries similar grit. Bran attempts to retrieve the cup if unwatched. Similar residue supports testimony; it is not an infallible assay.
What did Odran bring? Petition, refuge plan, outer stone and damaged professional ring in his chest. Lira recognizes the ring’s two repairs from copied work. His employer can later verify his commission.
What is under the hearth? Druven finds the blocked passage and inner threshold on examination. Painted plaster, draughts, old household routes and the petition independently identify a former refuge.
Where is the body? Washhouse tracks, lamp soot, shroud caught on a peg; a helper saw Lira carrying it. Question her without threat. She followed Bran’s advice, not a spontaneous madness.

The silver key opens the chest; the party can also force it in view of witnesses. Its lock does not require guessing a verse. Keep copies of essential information elsewhere if destructive handling ruins a page. The broken ring is identification, not five pieces scattered through symbolic puzzle rooms. Lies belong to speakers with motives; a failed check does not make the GM supply a false fact as objective truth.

Escalation through choices

Use three visible pressures. These are scenario clocks, not new character resources. Tell players what actions advance them. Do not tick them every time someone speaks.

Starts at one after the death. Advance for an assault, an accusation treated as permission to raid, or an armed message allowed through unanswered. At two, doors and escorts are contested; at four, a named faction attempts a specific attack or seizure. Guarded custody, a witnessed account and reaching Massen’s cousins can reduce it. The whole of Ubral does not automatically go to war.

Starts at zero. Advance for substantial delay without heating or shelter, damage to a shelter, or sending people across the ford unprepared. At two, infirm guests need relocation; at four, a specific stranded group needs immediate rescue. Fuel, blankets, paired guides and opening nearby buildings address it.

Starts at one when the matching stones meet. The default death advances it to two. Advance for moving a named guest towards expulsion, repeating the old refusal as a remedy, or opening the lower way without watchers and refuge prepared. At three, connected doors mislead and voices lure; at four, the keeper enacts the refusal physically. This is an intervention the party can interrupt, not instant irreversible defeat. Isolation of the stones can slow it but does not undo what has begun.

The dawn deadline is Bran’s demand that the household surrender Lira, not a universal expiration of guest-right. If the party exposes him early, change his tactics. He may seek protection, offer partial knowledge or escape; do not make him immune until his scheduled confession.

The muster and the ford

Massen’s men wait below the ford. They believe their people may be hostages. A witness from one of the excluded families is also waiting there with a surviving shelter token and a recent passage demand. The players can bring them in, meet outside, send an escort or arrange a second place of safety. The water cannot name the murderer. Its danger is current, weather and the pressure of armed men on narrow footing.

A lowland officer may offer blankets and escort in return for a garrison at the pass. This adds a useful but politically costly option, not a punishment for the party’s preference. Omit this complication in a short session.

What the haunting can and cannot do

Familiar voices draw people towards the sealed passage. Footsteps answer from empty rooms. The keeper withdraws a stool or empty bowl, then goes back to the bolt when someone calls outside. Watching her repeatedly reveals that she is both enforcing and caught in the refusal. She cannot give the killer’s name, pronounce guilt or narrate every century of history.

Keep a human route through the yard visible. Paired watches, shared sleeping rooms and answering voices only with a companion reduce danger. Characters can retreat to another sheltered building. A successful ward can protect an established boundary according to its own rules; it does not automatically dissolve this site’s history. Never invent a new patron fee after a player has accepted stated terms.

If a character attacks the keeper, let a suitable weapon or working drive her back or break her grasp. Ordinary weapons cannot kill a remnant, but can break a door, free caught clothing or sever a binding object. State this before asking for a pointless damage roll. She returns to the bolt while the passage remains denied. This is a hazard with an objective, not a creature with arbitrary immunity until five runes are stepped on.

Opening the refuge

The players need to establish three practical conditions:

  1. Current guests have somewhere safe to go and a route there. A promise of shelter is not enough if its door remains barred or the ford is impassable.

  2. Both threshold pieces are returned to their original positions and the old lower passage is opened. The outer piece is in Odran’s chest; the inner piece is beneath the bridal room. Examination, the plan and Lira’s account establish this without a single mandatory skill check.

  3. Living witnesses hold the route through the crossing. Nobody is expelled to stand in for the rejected dead. The keeper can lift the bolt when the refusal is no longer being performed by the household around her.

Opening the passage threatens the old chimney. Druven identifies this before the party commits. Earlier shoring, extra labour, controlled dismantling or established magic may preserve more of the house. Without those preparations, the bridal floor and hall roof become unsafe. Resolve the evacuation in distinct tasks: release the fitting, hold the lower door, carry wounded people, move a burning obstruction, reach a missing child. Do not repeat the same Arcana test until someone rolls well.

Bran may try to force Lira into the place after a relative frees him. This is preventable through secure custody or convincing his helper. His death does not appease the keeper. Let players arrest, disarm, injure or kill him as the confrontation actually unfolds, with the corresponding consequences. An NPC bride need not land the decisive blow.

Resolution guidance

Use the current Attribute plus Skill procedure. Useful approaches include Wits plus Insight for competing accounts, Wits plus Craft for the cup or supports, Wits plus Lore for local practice, Spirit plus Arcana for an established working, Presence plus Sway for reassurance, and Body plus Athletics for carrying or crossing. Choose the pair from the actual approach. Default to DV 3 under pressure; an ordinary unopposed observation needs no roll. Use DV 2 for a straightforward task, DV 4 for active resistance or a difficult physical constraint. State Dominant, Controlled or Desperate Position before the roll.

A miss can cost time, expose a witness, strain a support or worsen the declared danger. It need not destroy the clue. Story Beats are generated and spent under the core rules; failed DV 3 rolls do not automatically grant an NPC one SB, and nobody spends SB as a ten-minute rest fee. Apply Fatigue, harm and magical costs using the relevant existing procedure. Do not turn “one Fatigue” into an undefined week-long social penalty.

For physical opposition, use the current guard or raider adversary appropriate to the party rather than increasing every DV. Give opponents concrete aims: seize a witness, recover the cup, hold the gate. Retreat, surrender and changing allegiance are available. Before play, select the combat profiles from the campaign’s current adversary source; this scenario does not introduce a new monster stat block.

After the crossing

Separate four outcomes: the fate of the murdered guest’s case; the release or continued confinement of the dead; the safety of current guests; and the future of the marriage and pass. Success in one does not imply the others.

If the party opens the refuge, give the dead a departure and the living time to count people, dress injuries and find beds. No extra curse is required to make success costly. If they cannot open it tonight, evacuation and guarded containment can preserve lives while leaving a later return necessary. If they abandon people, record which people and who saw it; do not award automatic universal exile.

Bran can be held for a later witnessed judgment. Varrak’s authority is diminished by what is exposed. Displaced families retain claims the wedding never settled. Brin and Massen may marry later, separate, or revise their agreement. The party does not choose their marriage for them.

Rewards can be paid escort work, repairs, a named household’s hospitality or a safe-conduct actually recognized by its issuer. No blanket level of Social Capital with all Ubral is created. Odran’s daughter should receive his body or an honest account of its disposition, his personal effects and the evidence he carried.

Revision and source note

This revision replaces the contradictory assassin/protector identities, the scattered ring puzzle, the late Ward-Reader villain and the forced sacrifice. It retains the dead guest, wedding, chest, Bran and Lira. It draws on the Ubral setting and regional deck while treating the refuge’s binding as a local invention. The novel treatment fixes one first-person path through these events; player decisions remain authoritative in the adventure.
