🕯️ Between Knot and Gate

Tier II–III 3–4 Sessions Civic Horror / Bargain Drama Author: Nicholas A. Gasper Patrons: Morag the Hag · The Pale Shepherd

A borderland duchy's pilgrimage gate — the arch that has guided its dead to rest for four hundred years — is failing. The dead linger. Morag offers order through thorned bargains. The Pale Shepherd asks for vigil, memory and covenant instead. The party does not choose who wins. They choose what replaces the old order, and then live in it.

Reading an encounter badge. Each badge names a DV and a Position. It does not name a dice pool, and that is deliberate: the pool is Attribute + Skill, chosen from how the player says they are going about it. Two characters can attempt the same encounter with different pools and both be right. Where an approach is obviously the intended one, the badge says so; where it does not, ask what they are doing and build the pool from the answer.
The shape of it, for the GM

Hook. A noble heir walks back from a failed funeral procession with no memory of the last four days. That night, the hauntings start.

Two offers arrive within a day of each other. Kettle-Mae, Morag's envoy, will bind the duchy with thorned bargains: order by the end of the week, at a price nobody has read to the bottom of. Brother Tellen, the Shepherd's acolyte, wants the threshold restored properly — vigil, the gate's true name, a covenant — which is slow, and people are dying now.

Neither is a villain, and the adventure breaks if you play one as one. Morag's way works. That is the horror of it.

🕯️ Act I — Frayed Thresholds

Goals: learn what broke, meet both offers, and let the party feel the cost of waiting.

⚰️ The Broken Procession
A funeral fails at the old gate. The coffin will not pass under the arch; the bearers cannot explain why their arms stop. Then the lost begin to manifest, politely, one at a time, asking for directions.
Timer: Public Panic [4] Read the oath residue on the arch (DV 4, Controlled) Soothe the manifesting dead (DV 4, Desperate) Hold the crowd together (DV 3, Controlled)
Clean: the procession completes. The party has the crowd's trust for the rest of Act I, and one witness who saw the heir walk back.
Partial: the coffin passes, but three of the lost follow the party home and are still there in the morning. Tick Stalled Passing +1.
Miss: the crowd breaks. Tick Public Panic +2 and Stalled Passing +1. Someone is trampled, and it was avoidable.
🤝 Two Offers, One Cost
Kettle-Mae proposes a Rite of the Thorned Bargain: peace in the duchy by the week's end, in exchange for terms she will happily read aloud if asked. Brother Tellen proposes a Rite of the Memory Corral instead — find out why the gate fails before deciding what to do about it.
Read Kettle-Mae's terms honestly (DV 4, Controlled) Persuade the magistrate to wait (DV 4, Desperate)
GM note. Let a player read the bargain in full if they ask. It is not a trick contract; it is an honest one, and it is reasonable. That is what makes it dangerous.
Any outcome: if the party accept even a limited bargain here, tick Bargain Net Tightens +1 and note who signed.
🪧 The Crossroads Lead
A Rite of the Crossroads Whisper at the well, or a night of ordinary legwork, points two ways: the Oath-Ledgers in the Ledger Hall, or the Buried Doorframe under the Manor of Borrowed Faces. Both are true. Neither is sufficient.
Timer: Hidden Oath Cartel [6] Timer: Gate Repair or Replacement [8] Find the thread worth pulling (DV 3, Controlled)

🪢 Act II — The Knot Tightens, the Gate Groans

Goals: unravel the causes and let the party commit to a method. Run two or three of these threads, not all three.

📕 Thread: The Oath Cartel
A syndicate of guild factors and magistrates has been monetising funerals through the Rite of the Twisted Knot — selling passage the gate used to give away. They did not break the gate. They noticed it was broken before anyone else and priced accordingly.
Timer: Hidden Oath Cartel [6] The ledger heist (DV 4, Desperate) Bone-Truth interrogation (DV 4, Controlled) Challenge a thorned bargain in open court (DV 5, Desperate)
Clean: the ledgers name the Cartel's members and, in the margin, the date the gate first failed — two years before anyone admits.
Partial: the party get the names but are seen. Magistrate Thorn knows, and is not angry; he sends a clerk to make them a better offer.
Miss: a bargain is invoked against them. One PC is bound to a term they did not read. Tick Bargain Net +2.
🖼️ Thread: The Stolen Doorframe
A relic of the first gate hangs as décor in the Manor of Borrowed Faces, sanded and varnished and hung above a fireplace. It has spent sixty years absorbing a family's grief and it is very, very full.
Timer: Gate Repair or Replacement [8] Social infiltration under a borrowed face (DV 4, Controlled) A Memory Corral in the middle of a ballroom (DV 5, Desperate) Swear a Threshold Covenant with the family (DV 4, Controlled)
Clean: the family gives the frame up, and comes to the vigil. Tick Gate Repair +2.
Partial: they surrender the frame and nothing else. Tick Gate Repair +1; the family will testify for the Cartel later out of spite.
Miss: the Corral opens in a crowded room. Every guest recalls their worst grief at once. Tick Public Panic +2.
🌙 Thread: The Heir's Missing Memory
Heir Lysa lost four days, and inside them is the gate's true keeper-name. Recovering it means a dream vigil at the bedside of her dying tutor, and a crossing of the crypt beneath the Splintered Gate.
Timer: Gate Repair or Replacement [8] Dream vigil at the tutor's bedside (DV 4, Controlled) Crypt traversal under shadow-flock (DV 4, Desperate) Accept what the memory actually says (DV 4, Desperate)
The reveal, whichever way it goes. The keeper line is not lost. It was ended — the last keeper was bought out, quietly, by people who found a broken gate more profitable than a working one. Lysa is the rightful keeper, and has been all along, and nobody told her because telling her would have cost money.
Miss: she recovers the memory and it breaks something in her. She is still the keeper. She is now also a person who knows what was done to her.
Mid-act set pieces — use one

The Market of Masks. Morag's fair-night. Every stall sells a small bargain that solves a real problem the party currently has, cheaply and immediately. Nothing here is a trap. Everything here is a hook, and the price is always paid later by someone who is not at the fair.

The Vigil of Stillness. A Shepherd rite that holds one district quiet for a single night. It costs the party Fatigue and Obligation and it changes nothing structural. It buys a night. The point of the scene is that a night is worth something, and that it is not enough.

⚖️ Act III — Binding or Passing

Goal: settle the duchy. All three endings work. None of them is clean.

🪢 Bind the City (Morag)
A mass Thorned Bargain: safety in exchange for obedience, sworn publicly and enforced by an oath office that will still exist in a hundred years. The opposition is not a monster. It is a bureaucracy and a crowd.
Encounter: The Court of Oaths (DV 5, Desperate)
Result: the hauntings stop within a month. Markets calm. Funerals function. Tick Bargain Net to full deliberately — that is not a failure state here, it is the ending the party chose.
🕯️ Restore the Gate (Shepherd)
A Threshold Covenant sworn by a rightful keeper before a community that turns out to witness it. Needs the doorframe, water blessed by loss, and the keeper's name — and someone has to accept the cost, which is real and permanent and falls on a named person.
Requires: Gate Repair [8] filled Encounter: The Funeral March (DV 5, Desperate)
The Funeral March. Hundreds through the threshold in one night, in order, while the panic, the hazards and Kettle-Mae's people all work the queue. Not a fight. A logistics problem with grief in it.
Result: the gate holds. Someone the party knows carries the covenant's weight for the rest of their life.
⚖️ The Third Way
Time-boxed bargains that protect the weakest, with sunset clauses that expire when the gate is restored, under public oversight nobody has agreed to fund. It requires both patrons to accept half of what they wanted.
Encounter: Get both patrons to sign (DV 5, Desperate)
Result: civic legitimacy, partial favour from both, and a recurring auditor from each — the Silent Collector and Brother Tellen, arriving separately, asking the same questions.
Failure states

The gate collapses. Stalled Passing fills and stays full. The duchy becomes a liminal haunt: people still live there, and the dead still queue at the arch, and everyone has learned to step around them.

Oath-martial law. Bargain Net fills without the party choosing it. The Cartel wins by default, and the ending is Morag's ending without any of Morag's honesty.

👤 Key NPCs

Archivist Avena
Shepherd-leaning · the duchy's records office
Wants honest endings and has wanted them for thirty years. She knows the gate's old name and will not say it until she is sure what the party will do with it.
Magistrate Thorn
Morag-leaning · the Oath Cartel's respectable face
An efficient tyrant by paperwork, who genuinely believes the bargains are mercy — he has the mortality figures and they support him. He is never rude, never lies, and never once threatens anybody.
Heir Lysa
Centre · the missing four days
Wants her memory back, until she starts to suspect what is in it. She is the rightful threshold keeper and the last person in the duchy to find that out.
Kettle-Mae
Morag's envoy
Charming, funny, and completely honest about her terms. Cruel only when crossed, and then thoroughly. She likes the party and this will not save them.
Brother Tellen
The Shepherd's acolyte
Exhausted and stubbornly kind. He is right about what should be done and has no plan for how to do it in the time available, and he knows it.

📍 Locations

The Splintered Gate. A ruined arch on the pilgrim road. The candle-flames in its niches gutter upward. Names are whispered in it at a volume just below hearing.
The Ledger Hall. Thorn-carved lecterns, quills that drink, and four hundred years of oaths filed by date. The Cartel's books are here, in plain sight, correctly indexed.
The Wakehouse. Woven veils where the recently dead are laid out. Memory condenses on the glass like dew, and the attendants wipe it away each morning without discussing it.
The Manor of Borrowed Faces. A portrait gallery that watches back, above a fireplace with a very old doorframe around it.
The Crossroads Well. Mouth-stones for whispering into. Offerings tied on with knots that nobody in living memory has untied.

⚔️ Bestiary

Very little in this adventure can be solved by fighting it, and the two things that can are not the interesting parts.

One of the Lost — TL 2, Armor 0, Harm 1, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Undead (unquiet).
A soul that reached the arch and was turned back. Not malicious — stuck. It asks for directions, and it will ask again, and it does not understand why nobody answers properly.
SB Spends: 1 — Ask Again: it repeats its question to a PC, who takes −1 die on their next roll if they do not answer it. 2 — Draw a Crowd: three more arrive; tick Public Panic +1. 3 — Cold Grasp: Harm 1, and it takes a small memory of the way home.
Weakness: An honest answer. Tell it where it is and that the gate is broken, and it will stop and wait — which is worse to watch, and does not tick anything.
Cartel Enforcer — TL 4, Armor 1, Harm 3, Harm Levels: 3 (standard). Humanoid.
Guild men on retainer, carrying a writ that makes what they are doing legal. They would prefer to show you the writ.
SB Spends: 1 — Produce the Writ: the party are in the wrong, and any bystanders side with the Cartel. 2 — Invoke the Bargain: a PC bound by any thorned bargain acts at one Position worse this scene. 3 — Call the Magistrate: Thorn arrives, and he is perfectly reasonable.
Weakness: Paperwork cuts both ways. A contested writ stops them where a blade would not.
The Splintered Gate — TL 7, Harm Levels: None (puzzle). Place.
The adventure's actual antagonist is an arch that has forgotten its own name. It does not act, threaten, or resist. It simply does not work, and everything else in the duchy is a consequence of that.
SB Spends: 2 — The Queue Lengthens: tick Stalled Passing +1. 3 — It Almost Works: a funeral passes cleanly, and everyone concludes the problem has resolved itself. 4 — Backlash: every soul at the arch manifests at once, across a whole zone.
Resolution: Re-bind it or replace it. Re-binding needs the doorframe, water blessed by loss, and the keeper's true name, sworn by a rightful keeper before witnesses. Replacing it needs a bargain large enough to do the same work, and someone to hold the other end of it forever.

🎭 Resolution Epilogues

Order at a Price. The markets calm. Funerals function, on schedule, correctly filed. Within two winters nobody under twenty remembers it being any other way, and something has gone out of how people speak to each other that nobody can name.
Mercy with Scars. The hauntings end. Grief is honoured properly and publicly. The burden is real, it is carried by named people the party know, and it does not get lighter.
Precarious Balance. The civic oaths sunset on schedule. The gate tends itself. It will hold until next winter, and then somebody will have to decide all of this again — which is, in the end, what governing is.

Running note Position and DV are separate questions and should be set separately. DV is how hard the task is; Position is what it costs to fail. Most of this adventure sits at DV 4, Controlled, and gets its pressure from timers and from what the party have already signed — not from inflated numbers. Name the cost aloud before the roll, every time.
