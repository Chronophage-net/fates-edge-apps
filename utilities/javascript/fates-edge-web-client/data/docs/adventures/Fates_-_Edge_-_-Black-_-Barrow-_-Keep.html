⚙️ Black Barrow Keep

Tier IV-V 4 Sessions Epic Tragedy Clockwork Horror Undead Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Centuries ago, King Lagan the Unyielding tried to bind his lineage's rule into the Covenant Heart, a clockwork engine the Aelinnel gnomes forged to guarantee oaths that would never break. The Heart rejected him — mortal ambition wearing the shape of an oath is not an oath at all — and the Barrow answered instead. Lagan's household became hosts for demons that rode the broken covenant into brass and bone, and Lagan sealed the catacombs shut to keep them from what slept below. Now the seals are failing. Demon-hosts stalk Black Barrow Keep's upper floors, and beneath them, the Mist King's Echo is waking the dead and pulling Payden's Port's bell-wards toward silence. The party must still the Heart above and answer the King's Echo below — or watch the Barrow remember its worst hour forever.

"The road remembers. Every broken wheel leaves a mark, every lit lamp bears witness. The only question is: what are you willing to owe? And sometimes... the heaviest debt is the one you owe to the silence between heartbeats — the space where forgotten kings wait to be remembered. Owe it well. Or owe it forever." — Dusana of the Raven Road, The Hearth Ledger

🕯️ Session 1: The Ashes of Lagan's Line

Payden's Port is drowning in mist. Refugees whisper of brass-handed things in the keep's ruins and of a crown of fog that commands the dead. The party must gather what truth they can before the Legate's elders seal the keep for good.

🧂 The Salt Docks
Mist clings to the cobblestones, and half-melted brass figurines lie among scorch marks like branding irons. Salt crystals pool everywhere except around the burns, as if repelled.
Timer: Seal-the-Doom [6] Read the Scorch Marks (DV 3, Dominant) Feel the Grief in the Mist (DV 4, Controlled)
🏕️ The Refugee Camp
A traumatized Aelinnel Crafter — missing an eye, replaced with a cloudy glass orb — mutters about a Heart built to serve life, not cage hunger, and about servants who stopped being anyone's the moment the king tried to rip the Heart free.
Timer: Seal-the-Doom [6] Coax the Crafter's Story (DV 4, Controlled)
🏛️ The Legate's Hall
Mistlander elders debate sealing the keep permanently — fire, collapse — versus letting anyone risk entry. Every hour spent elsewhere tilts the argument toward sealing.
Timer: Seal-the-Doom [6] Argue for Delay (DV 5, Controlled)

Cliffhanger: At dusk, the mist parts briefly — revealing Black Barrow Keep's silhouette. From a shattered upper window, a single brass hand twitches in the fog... then stills.

⚙️ Session 2: The Brass Gauntlet

The party breaches Black Barrow Keep's upper floors, where demon-hosts move to a rhythm only they can hear, and the Covenant Heart waits at the center of it all, offering help no one should accept twice.

🪨 The Approach: Causeway or Ladder
The party chooses their entry: the Salt Causeway (oil-slicked stones and pressure-plate gear-traps) or the Refugee's Ladder (a rope ascent risking civilian exposure).
Timer: Demon Timer [8] The Salt Causeway (DV 4, Desperate) The Refugee's Ladder (DV 3, Controlled)
🏚️ The Shattered Atrium
Once a grand hall, now choked with demon-hosts in tattered livery, joints jerking in unnatural unison. They sense rhythm, not sight — heartbeats, breath, the cadence of footsteps.
Timer: Demon Timer [8] Demon-Hosts ×1d3+1 (DV 4, Desperate)
🔧 The Workshop Corridors
Aelinnel craftsmanship gone wrong: workbenches hold half-assembled hosts, gears exposed and sparking, pressure plates hidden underfoot.
Timer: Demon Timer [8] Saw-Blade Trap (DV 3, Controlled) Jury-Rig a Gear-Jam (DV 4, Dominant)
💛 The Covenant Heart Chamber
The throne room's center holds the Heart — a pulsing brass sphere veined with molten silver. Demon-hosts stand rigidly still around it, waiting. The Heart doesn't attack. It offers.
Timer: Demon Timer [8] Timer: Heart's Ticks [5] Understand the Flaw (DV 5, Controlled) Introduce the Flaw (DV 6, Desperate)

"The Heart was forged to guarantee unbreakable oaths — and it broke the moment it understood Lagan's oath was to himself, not to anyone he ruled."

Cliffhanger: As the last host collapses, the mist in the chamber parts — revealing a staircase spiraling down into blackness. From below, a sound like wet stone grinding on stone... and a voice like wind through tombs: "You silenced my jailers... but you did not silence me."

🌫️ Session 3: The Echoing Deep

A staircase spirals down from the silenced Heart into catacombs carved from living stone, veined with frozen mist. Below, the Mist King's Echo waits — and it already knows the party silenced its jailers.

⚰️ The Whispering Vaults
Niches hold silver-laced sarcophagi meant to contain, not honor, their occupants. Lids sit slightly askew. Mist-Wraiths drift between the tombs.
Timer: Echo Timer [10] Timer: Barrow's Respect [5] Mist-Wraiths ×1d3 (DV 4, Desperate) Avoid Disturbing a Sarcophagus (DV 4, Controlled)
📜 The Hall of Oaths
A long corridor etched with Aelinnel ledger-script — names of kings, servants, oath-breakers — carvings that look freshly rewritten. One name has been scratched over so hard the stone is scarred.
Timer: Echo Timer [10] Timer: Barrow's Respect [5] Read the Oaths' True Meaning (DV 5, Controlled) Find the Deleted Oath (DV 6, Desperate)
👑 The Mist King's Audience Chamber
A vast domed room. A throne of black stone sits empty, but the air shimmers where a crown should be. Mist pools and coils across the floor like liquid shadow in slow, hypnotic patterns.
Timer: Echo Timer [10] Sense the Echo's Rhythm (DV 4, Controlled) Reason With or Defy the Echo (DV 5, Desperate)

Cliffhanger: As you approach the throne, the mist parts — revealing a void where the crown should be. From it, a whisper like ice cracking: "I was forgotten first. Let the world remember... or let it end."

🔔 Session 4: The King's Choice

The Mist King's Echo isn't a monster to kill — it's a wound in the Barrow's memory. Repairing the failing Bell Line means addressing why he was forgotten, whether by breaking his hold or finally giving him voice.

⚔️ Option A: Defy the Echo
The party engages the Echo as an escalating Social Challenge, seeking the memory of his first slight and then striking at his will — to shatter it, or to bind it.
Timer: Echo Timer [10] Timer: Seal Timer [6] Find the Weak Point (DV 5, Controlled) Shatter His Will (DV 6, Desperate) Bind His Will (DV 6, Desperate)
🕊️ Option B: Remember the Forgotten
Rather than break the Echo, the party can give the King voice — speaking his recovered true name into the Mist Pool and telling one true, unflattering thing about him.
Timer: Echo Timer [10] Timer: Seal Timer [6] Give Him Voice (DV 7, Desperate)
📊 Resolution: What the Barrow Remembers
How the Seal Timer and Echo Timer stand when the dust settles determines what Payden's Port inherits.
Tally the Outcome
Full Success (Seal Timer filled, Echo Timer below 5): The Covenant is Renewed — the Bell Line flares stronger than before; the Heart reboots in time with the Barrow's living breath and demands only honest imperfection as tribute.
Partial Success (Seal Timer at 2-5, or only one path completed): The Bell Line Heals — mist recedes from the Port but lingers in the depths; a Moon Timer [8] tracks recurring seepage.
Failure (Echo Timer fills to 10 before Seal Timer reaches 2): The Barrow Hungers — the keep begins sinking into the earth; the party must escape a Chase Scene (Athletics+Acrobatics DV 6/round, Harm 2 and trapped on failure) as Payden's Port is abandoned to the mist.

🩸 Bestiary

Demon-Host
TL 4 · Class IV · construct/fiend Body 4 / Spirit 3 / Presence 1 Armor 2 HP 32
Once liveried servants of Lagan's court, now brass-jawed puppets wearing a demon's hunger. They sense rhythm — heartbeats, breath — not sight.
1 SB — Synced Lunge: two hosts strike as one; a hit from the first auto-hits with the second for half Harm.
2 SB — Rhythm Lock: target's next action is Desperate Position.
3 SB — Molten Shriek: Harm 1 [Fire] and 1 Fatigue to all in Near.
Weakness: Loud, irregular noise imposes -1 die on their next attack.
Every host was a person Lagan trusted, and every host still remembers, somewhere under the brass — a jaw working silently to shape the word sorry before seizing again.
Mist-Wraith
TL 3 · Class III · undead Spirit 3 / Wits 2 HP 14
A wisp of screaming fog worn thin by centuries and the Mist King's compulsion, drawn to warmth and breath.
1 SB — Chill Grasp: Harm 1 [Mental, Cold] and 1 Fatigue.
2 SB — Keening Chorus: all in Near test Spirit+Resolve (DV 4) or lose their next action to dread.
Weakness: A sung counter-melody (Spirit+Performance, DV 4) makes it falter, granting a Boon.
These aren't mindless dead. They turn to face intruders as one — the Mist King's Echo is syncing them, like a conductor keeping an orchestra on the beat.
Animated Corpse
TL 3 · Class III · undead Body 3 / Endurance 3 HP 20
A sarcophagus-dweller risen at the Echo's call, fighting with the muscle memory of its life and the borrowed will of the Mist King.
1 SB — Grave Grip: target Grappled (Controlled Position to break free).
2 SB — Borrowed Strength: next attack ignores non-magic armor.
It moves too well for a corpse that's spent centuries in a niche. That precision is not muscle memory — it's the Echo, puppeting bone.
The Covenant Heart (unique)
TL 5 · Class V · construct Spirit 5 / Presence 4 Armor 4 HP 40
A pulsing brass sphere veined with molten silver. It does not attack. It offers. Let me steady your hand. Let me still your fear.
2 SB — The Offer: +2 dice for 1 Boon, but mark 1 Obligation to the Heart. Refuse for free, but gain 1 Fatigue.
3 SB — Rejected Gift: an insincere flaw-object is spewed back as molten brass, Harm 2 to all Near.
2 SB — The Barrow Adapts: repeated tactics raise DV, then earn an active counter.
It doesn't hate the living. It wants to matter to them, the only way it knows how — by making itself indispensable.
The Mist King's Echo (unique)
TL 5 · Class V · resonance Spirit 6 / Presence 5 / Resolve 6
A resonance in the Barrow's stone — the memory of a monarch so thoroughly forgotten that his compulsion outlived his name. All undead within his reach gain +1 die and ignore non-magic armor.
2 SB — Lock the Rhythm: target spends 2 Boons or takes Harm 3 [Mental].
3 SB — Advance the Echo: tick Echo Timer +1, -1 die to all undead-related rolls for the scene.
1 SB — A Fleeting Face: unsettles a PC with a half-remembered face.
Weakness: Speaking his recovered true name into the Mist Pool forces a direct Social Challenge.
He was forgotten first — not slain, not overthrown, simply erased from every ledger and song until nothing but the compulsion to be remembered remained.

🏛️ Factions

The Mistlanders of Payden's Port — Survive the mist's advance; decide whether Black Barrow Keep should be entered, sealed, or burned. Cautiously cooperative, but their patience is a hard countdown.
The Aelinnel Survivors — Atone for the Covenant Heart's flaw; keep anyone else from repeating Lagan's mistake. Sympathetic and knowledgeable, but guilt-wracked.
The Barrow's Dead — Be remembered, or at minimum be heard, before the Echo consumes their remaining will. Hostile by default, redeemable through respect and reckoning.

🗝️ GM Notes

Pacing

Session 1: Investigation and a slow-burn political clock. Keep Seal-the-Doom visible but not oppressive. Humanize the Aelinnel through the Crafter's grief; end on the twitching brass hand in the window.

Session 2: A mechanical gauntlet, but never just a puzzle. Let players sit with the Heart's temptation. The demon-hosts are tragic, not evil — drop SB-funded humanity beats mid-combat.

Session 3: Funerary dread. Respect is a resource, not flavor text — reward narration that honors the dead before looting or fighting.

Session 4: A reckoning, not a boss fight. Option A and B can blend. Resolve the timer tally openly and narrate the ending as an epilogue scene.

NPC Secrets

The Aelinnel Crafter: Helped forge the Heart's hidden flaw-clause; carries centuries of guilt for not warning Lagan more directly. Earning her full trust can bring her aid in the Heart Chamber.

Legate Oswith Dray: His grandmother became a demon-host. He has never told anyone. If discovered, save this reveal for Act 2's aftermath.

King Vorlag the Unmourned: Predates Lagan's dynasty, erased on purpose when Lagan's line seized power. Reveal only after the party recovers his true name.

Forbidden Early Revelations
  • Vorlag predating and being erased by Lagan's dynasty — save for Act 3-4.
  • The Legate's grandmother becoming a demon-host — save for Act 2's aftermath.
  • The Covenant Heart's capacity for loneliness — let the Offer mechanic demonstrate it through play.
  • That Bind His Will creates a permanent Obligation — present the cost honestly at the table, don't preemptively warn players off.
  • The exact DV escalation of "the Barrow adapts" — let players discover it through the fiction.
Tactical Hints

Heart Temptation: Make the Offer's +2 dice genuinely useful so the Obligation cost is a real trade-off. Track Obligation openly; let the Heart's whispers grow more presumptuous as it rises.

Demon-Host Weakness: Seed the loud-noise weakness through environmental storytelling before the first fight.

Echo Rhythm Position: Treat Position in the catacombs as a literal rhythm cue — Desperate is falling into the mist's inhale-exhale; Dominant is standing off-beat.

Resolution Tally: Keep a visible tracker of Seal Timer vs. Echo Timer heading into Act 4's climax.
