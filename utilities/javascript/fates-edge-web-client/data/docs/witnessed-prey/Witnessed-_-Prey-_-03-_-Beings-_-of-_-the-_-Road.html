Beings of the Road

The Witnessed Prey, a Hunter's Guide — Chapter 3

“Miss your footing and the road gives you another chance farther downhill.”—Dusana of the Raven Road (apocryphal)

Not every hungry thing wears a ghost’s shroud or a demon’s crown. Some are simply travelers who forgot to stop, thresholds that learned to walk, or debts that grew teeth. I met the Hollowed Stag in a rain-rutted lane south of Thepyrgos; it did not charge, it only watched. When I offered a memory of my mother’s kitchen, it dipped its antlers and left an acorn that later cured a child’s fever. The road keeps its own reckoning, and these are its irregular entries.

This chapter catalogs the wanderers you might meet between towns—creatures that guard no tomb, serve no master, and owe no patron. They are the road’s own children: lonely, patient, and always hungry for something you did not know you carried.

The Hollowed Stag (Forest Omen)

A stag with antlers of bleached bone, ribs visible through translucent hide. It never eats. It only walks.

  • TL: 3 (lone)
  • Harm: 3
  • Harm Levels: 3 (standard).
  • Timers: Silent Approach [4]—when ticks, the stag appears behind someone.
  • Tags: [BEAST] [SPIRIT] [DREAD]
  • Twist: It does not attack first. It watches. If you flee, the timer advances. If you stand still, it passes. If you offer a memory, it leaves a single acorn that cures one poison.

Character Inspiration: Two Cards on the Road

When creating a road-weary traveler (PC or NPC), draw two playing cards. Combine a Concept (Heart/Diamond) with a Complication (Spade/Club).

2–3: Exiled noble 2–3: Hunted by a rival
4–5: Grizzled scout4–5: Carries a cursed heirloom
6–7: Street-wise fixer6–7: Owe a favor to a crime lord
8–9: Failed scholar8–9: Secretly ill (timer [4])
10–J: Faith healer10–J: Witness to a forbidden rite
Q–K: Retired soldierQ–K: Spouse missing (search timer)
A: Last of a bloodlineA: Marked by a spirit (visible)

Then roll or pick a third card for a Goal (any suit):

  • Red (Heart/Diamond): Protect someone / find a treasure.
  • Black (Spade/Club): Destroy a rival / avenge an insult.
  • Ace: Discover the truth about [a place, death, or omen].

Example: 6♦ (Grizzled scout) + 8♣ (Secretly ill) + Q♠ (avenge an insult) = a sick tracker who wants revenge on the poacher who killed her mentor.

I keep a worn deck in my saddlebag for nights when the road offers no answers. The cards always give me a direction—even if it is the wrong one. That is still a direction.Lerris Fenwood
Do not trust a traveler who has never drawn a card. They are either lying or already dead.The Gray Wanderer
The road is a shuffle. The encounter is the draw. The twist is the debt.Saikou Ira
And the story? The story is what you do with the card you did not expect.Dusana
