The Cursed & Corrupted

The Witnessed Prey, a Hunter's Guide — Chapter 4

“Malachai does not curse. She offers gifts that remember to be hungry. The coin that never spends is the one you don’t remember taking.” —The Gray Wanderer

The curse is not a punishment. Malachai’s gifts are not evil. They are desperate. They are the answer you receive when you ask for help from the wrong threshold. This section catalogs those who have accepted a gift they cannot return: lycanthropes, vampires, slashers, memory-eaters, and the hollowed things that wander the cursed city.

Lycanthropes—The Shape of Hunger

Lycanthropy is a spiritual infection—a fragment of Malachai’s chained essence passed from host to host through violence, but only if the victim is in a state of profound emotional vulnerability.

Linn Sea-Wolf

Transforms into a massive seal or walrus. Solitary, melancholic, preys on ships.

  • TL 6. Shapechanger, Amphibious, Solitary.
  • Harm: 5. Fatigue: Body 4.
  • Harm Levels: 8 (advanced).
  • Traits: Claw (DV 2, Harm 2), Drowning Grasp (DV 4, Harm 1 + Suffocation). Silver Vulnerability (+2 Harm from silver). Transformation Trigger—first winter storm after a kill.
  • Clock: Moon’s Hunger [6]—when filled, the lycanthrope enters a frenzy.
  • Resolution: Silver weapon to the heart or brain. Aconite poultice can prevent transformation if applied within the hour.

Ykrul Sky-Hound

Hawk-headed hunter that transforms during the day. Hunts oath-breakers, not flesh.

  • TL 6. Shapechanger, Aerial, Solitary.
  • Harm: 4. Fatigue: Body 3.
  • Harm Levels: 8 (advanced).
  • Traits: Claw (DV 2, Harm 1), Blood Scent—automatically detects oath-breakers within Far. Silver Vulnerability (+2 Harm from silver). Transformation Trigger—sight of blood on fresh snow.
  • Clock: Blood Debt [6]—ticks each time the Hound hunts without finding an oath-breaker. When full, it turns on its own pack.
  • Resolution: Silver weapon, or a public confession of the oath broken.

Vampires—The First Children

Vampires are the eldest children of Malachai, the first to drink from the Chained Angel’s wound. The Thirteen First Vampires still walk the world.

Lesser Vampire (Bloodline)

  • TL 6. Undead, Hypnotic Gaze, Blood Drain.
  • Harm: 5. Fatigue: Body 4.
  • Harm Levels: 8 (advanced).
  • Traits: Claw (DV 2, Harm 1), Blood Drain (DV 3, Harm 1 + 1 Fatigue to victim, heals vampire 1 Harm). Sunlight Vulnerability—1 Harm per round in direct sun. Invitation Required—cannot enter a home without an invitation.
  • Clock: Thirst [6]—when filled, the vampire loses control and feeds openly.
  • Resolution: Stake through the heart (DV 5, must be wooden), decapitation, fire.

Other Children of Malachai

The Unkillable Killer (Slasher)

A mortal who bargained for invincibility. The curse: they cannot stop killing.

  • TL 6. Corporeal, Regenerating, Hungry.
  • Harm: 6. Fatigue: Body 4.
  • Harm Levels: 8 (advanced); only silver, fire, or a rite at the first victim’s grave counts against this pool — mundane Harm does not reduce it.
  • Traits: Regeneration—ignore first 2 Harm each scene. Unkillable—cannot die from mundane harm. The Face in the Mirror—once per session, GM may force a Endurance test (DV 4) or suffer –1 die to all rolls for the scene.
  • Clock: Hunger Timer [6]—ticks each time the killer kills a sentient being. At 3, social rolls –1 die. At 6, frenzy.
  • Resolution: Silver, fire, or finding the grave of the first victim.

The Memory Eater (Psychic Vampire)

A scholar who wanted to know everything; a lover who wanted to never forget. The curse: they must feed on fresh memories or lose their own.

  • TL 5. Corporeal, Telepathic, Hungry.
  • Harm: 3. Fatigue: Spirit.
  • Harm Levels: 3 (standard).
  • Traits: Memory Feed—touch a willing or helpless target, take one specific memory; target forgets permanently; gain 1 Boon. Phasing—intangible 1 round per scene.
  • Clock: Hunger for Emotion [6]—must feed on a significant memory once per week or lose 1 Fatigue per day.
  • Resolution: Feeding on a false memory disorients it.

Curses as Architecture (GM Tool)

A curse from Malachai is not a spell; it is a rule inserted into the world. Write it like a clause in a contract:

“Until a silver coin that never spends is returned to the salt pan, the one bound cannot drink fresh water. The water will turn to brine in their mouth.”

  • Timer: Mounting Toll [6]—ticks each time the one bound ignores the curse. When full, the curse spreads to a loved one.
  • Counter: Return the coin, or find a witness who saw the original bargain.
