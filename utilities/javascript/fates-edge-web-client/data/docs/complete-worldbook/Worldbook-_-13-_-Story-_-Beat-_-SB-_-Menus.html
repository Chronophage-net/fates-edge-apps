Story Beat (SB) Menus

The Edgewalker, compiler: Use a complication to expose a dependency the characters can act upon. An expensive consequence is not a substitute for a clear one. For clues drawn from the Ninth Words, use the passages and worked scene beside the fragment.

Universal SB Spend Menu

  • 1 SB — Shave time/position: add +1 segment to the current timer or drop Position one step or impose a small fee/toll.

  • 2 SB — Escalate pressure: upgrade the active ♣ or force a choice of Diamonds or tick a Front by 1.

  • 3 SB — Lock a gate/taboo: close a route until a named ♢ is spent or invoke a regional taboo.

  • 4+ SB — Reshape the leg: rewrite the route, start a new complication timer, demand immediate payment on a Debt.

Caravan Intent Dial

When traveling the overland routes of the eastern and southern realms, choose one intent per leg:

Speed

Press mounts and crew; risk lameness or exhaustion. Gain +1 Speed, but mark 1 Fatigue on a Miss.

Stealth

Avoid bandits and inspectors. Gain +1 die to Stealth rolls, but lose 1 segment of progress.

Show

Fly banners and announce your presence. Gain +1 Position on social rolls at the destination, but attract a rival’s attention (GM gains 1 SB).

Tribute

Travel as a bonded caravan of a patron. Gain protection (first hazard is ignored), but pay 1 Measure or a String to the patron’s factor.
