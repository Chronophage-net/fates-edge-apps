Saikou Ira's Compendium of the Veil — Chapter 9

Creature Stat Blocks

For use with the Fate’s Edge system. These statistics are drawn from Saikou’s case files.

Echo (Class I Spirit)
TL:1
Traits:Incorporeal, Repetitive, Trapped
Attacks:None (cannot harm directly)
Complications:Distraction (SB: 1 to create a sensory illusion), Delay (SB: 2 to cause a time loop)
Resolution:Witness a truth about the death (DV 3) to banish permanently.
Timers:Echo’s Loop [4] — when filled, the Echo expands its range.
Harm Levels:None (puzzle) — resolved by witness, not violence.
Anchor (Class II Spirit)
Tier: II–III
Traits:Incorporeal, Intelligent, Bound
Attacks:Manifestation (DV 3, Fatigue 1), Poltergeist Shove (DV 4, Harm 1)
Complications:Bargain Required — cannot be fought; must negotiate.
Resolution:Fulfill the Anchor’s unfinished business, or perform a witnessed substitution ritual (DV 4).
Timers:Anchor’s Patience [6] — when filled, the Anchor becomes a Poltergeist.
Harm Levels:3 (standard) at Cap 2–3; 8 (advanced) at Cap 4.
Poltergeist (Class IV Spirit)
Tier: III–IV
Traits:Incorporeal, Raging, Hungry
Attacks:Telekinetic Throw (DV 3, Harm 2), Possession Attempt (DV 4, Spirit+Resist or Controlled)
Complications:Aura of Fear (all actions Desperate within Near range), Environmental Collapse (timers advance)
Resolution:Containment ritual (The Sentinel) followed by negotiation with the underlying Anchor.
Timers:Poltergeist Escalation [8] — when filled, the spirit reaches Stage Four.
Harm Levels:8 (advanced).

Ghoul (Starved Anchor)

Tier: II–III
Traits:Corporeal, Hungry, Intelligent
Attacks:Claw (DV 2, Harm 1 + Fatigue 1), Bite (DV 3, Harm 2 + poison)
Complications:Regeneration (ignore first 2 Harm each scene), Ravenous (must feed or frenzy)
Resolution:Silver weapon to the heart, or fire. Negotiation possible if the ghoul is fed.
Timers:Hunger [6] — when filled, the ghoul attacks indiscriminately.
Harm Levels:3 (standard) at Cap 2–3; 8 (advanced) at Cap 4.

Lycanthrope (Linn Sea-Wolf)

TL:3
Traits:Shapechanger, Amphibious, Solitary
Attacks:Claw (DV 2, Harm 2), Drowning Grasp (DV 4, Harm 1 + Suffocation)
Complications:Silver Vulnerability (+2 Harm from silver), Transformation Trigger (first winter storm after a kill)
Resolution:Silver weapon to the heart or brain. Aconite poultice can prevent transformation if applied within the hour.
Timers:Moon’s Hunger [6] — when filled, the lycanthrope enters a frenzy.
Harm Levels:8 (advanced).

Vampire (Lesser Bloodline)

TL:3
Traits:Undead, Hypnotic Gaze, Blood Drain
Attacks:Claw (DV 2, Harm 1), Blood Drain (DV 3, Harm 1 + 1 Fatigue to victim, heals vampire 1 Harm)
Complications:Sunlight Vulnerability (1 Harm per round in direct sun), Invitation Required (cannot enter a home without invitation)
Resolution:Stake through the heart (DV 5, must be wooden), decapitation, fire.
Timers:Thirst [6] — when filled, the vampire loses control and feeds openly.
Harm Levels:8 (advanced).
Demon (Class C Possessor)
TL:4
Traits:Incorporeal (unless possessing), Intelligent, Malicious
Attacks (possessing):Use host’s physical stats +2, plus fear aura (DV 3, Fear effect)
Complications:Cannot be harmed directly while possessing; exorcism required.
Resolution:The Sentinel’s exorcism ritual (DV 5, requires three witnesses and the demon’s true name).
Timers:Possession Depth [8] — when filled, the host is permanently erased.
Harm Levels:None while possessing (puzzle) — the host’s body has ordinary Harm Levels, but the demon itself is only removed by exorcism.
