Witchcraft

Core Rules Expansion Magic System Familiars Author: Nicholas A. Gasper

Witchcraft is Fate's Edge's system for reciprocal, intimate magic — power drawn from thresholds, Names, spirits, debts, and vows that can answer back. This document covers the core Witchcraft procedure, the Prices a witch pays, Identity Strain, the Observances that keep a witch whole, Universal Workings and Selected Rites, the five Witch Orders and their signature rites, the full rules for binding and using a familiar, and the Witchcraft follower subsystem. Use it whenever a PC or notable NPC practices witchcraft, binds a familiar, or serves a witch's patron.

Preamble

There are places where the world forgets its laws — doorways no one admits to knocking upon, Names that rewrite the one who dares to speak them. Shadows are not evil. They are consequences given shape: the costs a person refuses to tally, the truths they bury, the debts that return to collect themselves.

Witchcraft is reciprocal and intimate. A working binds the witch to something that can answer back: a threshold, a spirit, a vow, a Name, a patron, a memory, a debt.

Core Principles

Shadows

Shadows arise wherever truth is denied, debt goes unacknowledged, or identity fractures under unmet need. They are narrative pressure given teeth — the world insisting that what was hidden eventually be paid for.

Thresholds

A threshold is any place where a state changes: waking to dreaming, innocence to agency, belonging to exile, life to echo. Crossing one invites witness — and witnesses demand accounting.

Name-Truth

A Name is not a label. It is a claim with weight. Names can be protected, traded, buried, sharpened, dulled, or stolen. When a working touches a Name, it touches the self behind it.

Witchcraft Procedure

Casting a Working

When you attempt a working, state the Intent, the Vector, and the Price.

  • Intent: what changes (veil, bind, unmake, reveal, curse, sever, restore).
  • Vector: what carries the change (name, oath, threshold, shadow, dream, debt, token, bloodless offering).
  • Price: what you pay (Shadow, Shame, Identity).

Then roll Attribute + Witchcraft vs. DV set by scope.

Scope and Difficulty

ScopeExamplesDV
Subtle / PersonalBlur attention, soften a memory-edge, silence a doorway, hide a token.2
SituationalBind a promise, distort a reflection, mark a Name, hinder pursuit.3
Scene-ShapingCompel a confession, veil a crowd, turn a gathering into a threshold.4
Identity-AlteringRewrite a role, bury a name-right, sever a bond, harvest an echo.5+

Outcomes

  • Full Success: The working takes hold as intended. Pay the chosen price.
  • Partial Success: The working takes hold with a complication. Pay the price and the GM adds a consequence.
  • Miss: The working fails or twists. Pay the price and the GM escalates the consequence.

Push It

Before the roll, or after a partial success, you may Push It to increase scope or intensity. Choose one:

  • Mark +1 Shadow and gain +1d.
  • Mark +1 Shame and gain +1 Effect.
  • Mark +1 Identity Strain and reduce the DV by 1 (minimum 2).

Prices

Every working demands a price. Choose one unless a rite specifies otherwise.

Shadow
  • You accrue spiritual residue and narrative pressure.
  • The GM may introduce shadow-tells: cold glass, wrong reflections, trailing whispers, predatory coincidences.
Shame
  • You accept an internal consequence: a Condition, a vow-compulsion, a visible hesitation, a ritual aversion.
  • The GM may invoke Shame when a past choice becomes socially or spiritually relevant.
Identity
  • You relinquish a piece of self: a cherished memory dulled, a role surrendered, a name-right compromised, a bond weakened.
  • Identity prices always change what the character can say with full certainty about who they are.

Identity Strain

Identity Strain tracks cumulative erosion from rites that touch the self directly.

Identity Strain is a 4-step track. Mark it when a working severs, rewrites, harvests, or binds a Name; when you Push It with Identity; or when a rite demands it.

  • At 4, you must resolve strain at the end of the scene: roll Spirit + Endurance vs. DV 4.
  • Success: Clear 1 Identity Strain.
  • Failure: The GM chooses or offers a hard bargain: a memory blurs, a bond becomes conditional, a Name becomes buried, or an echo-self becomes active.

Observances

Observances are the lived rules witches follow to keep magic from turning inward and hollowing them out.

Common Observances

  • Count your debts aloud once per week at a threshold you respect.
  • Do not cross the same threshold twice in one night unless invited.
  • Never speak a Name in anger if you intend to keep it.
  • Leave an offering when a working succeeds cleanly: a coin, a braid of grass, a thread, a pinch of ash.
  • Do not take from the dead without a witness. The witness may be a spirit, a person, or the threshold itself.

Order Observances

The Shed Vigil (rites of the Ash-Kin of Isoka)

Signs: Rings of shed bone or horn; cuts on wrists marked with ash; small piles of discarded tokens at crossroads; masks split vertically, bound by cord.

Etiquette

  • Do not refuse a witch's request for a symbolic offering.
  • Do not speak someone's buried name aloud.
  • Accept the loss you cannot hide — loss defines you.
  • Never interrupt a Severance Rite.
Covenant Observances (Lunera)

Bargains and Prices

  • Memory Tithe: Offer a cherished memory — not erased, but dulled for you and sharpened for another.
  • Hidden Truth: Reveal a secret that would transform someone's understanding of their past.
  • Kept Silence: Maintain a night of absolute silence at a chosen threshold; break it and the next omen arrives twisted.
  • Selective Sight: In exchange for perfect clarity in one specific matter, accept permanent blind spots elsewhere (agree on 1–2 narrative blind spots).

Universal Workings

These are patterns of craft shared across Orders; the difference lies in doctrine, taboo, and cost.

Veil Working

Hide, blur, silence, soften, misdirect.

  • On success, gain +1d on the next stealth, deception, or evasion roll in the scene.
  • On partial, the veil works but leaves a tell: a reflection moves late, a shadow points wrong, a witness feels watched.

Binding Working

Hold, anchor, compel, constrain, mark.

  • On success, impose a constraint that must be narrated and testable ("cannot speak the lie," "cannot cross the doorway," "must answer the question").
  • On partial, the constraint holds but spawns a counter-pressure (a timer, a spirit's notice, a rival claim).

Unmaking Working

Break oaths, dissolve lesser magic, strip labels, undo a threshold knot.

  • On success, remove one active magical effect, one binding tag, or one scene-level occult constraint.
  • On miss, something else unravels instead (GM choice): an unrelated oath frays, a Name loosens, a memory bleeds into the present.

Crisis Working

Forbidden intensity. The working changes the entire scene.

  • Mark +2 Identity Strain.
  • On success, the scene changes as declared.
  • On partial, it changes — and a second consequence arrives with it.
  • On miss, the working becomes a hungry thing: it demands resolution through a timer, a bargain, or a witness.

Selected Rites

Rites are learned patterns with fixed doctrine. Each rite lists its tag, scope, and requirements.

Rite of the Ashen Knife

SEVERLow4 XP

Type: Action   Materials: Ash; a token willingly surrendered.

Effect: Create a knife of ash and intent for the scene.

  • Gain +1 Position when breaking bindings.
  • On a success, you may symbolically "cut" a minor hindrance or complication.

Price: Shadow or Identity.

Push It: Mark +1 Identity Strain to cut a more meaningful constraint (GM sets the boundary).

Rite of Echoed Dreams

ECHOStandard7 XP

Type: Action; Close   Materials: A shared token from a past moment (locket, scrap of letter, shard of glass).

Effect: Draw out a vivid waking-dream from a willing subject. Ask up to two:

  • "Who do you most fear becoming?"
  • "What moment do you wish you could change?"
  • "What truth do you not dare to look at?"

The dream answers in symbols; the GM ties each symbol to a concrete NPC, place, or timer. You gain +1d when acting with compassion or leverage on that truth this session.

Push It: You may enter the dream with them; both gain +1d to resist fear or confusion linked to that memory this session, and you mark +1 Obligation to the relevant patron or witness.

Hex of the Clinging Shadow

HEXStandard8 XP

Type: Scene; Near   Materials: Fragment of the target's reflection (hair from their comb, a sketch of their face).

Effect: Call a second shadow to cling to the target for the scene (or until dispelled).

  • They cannot fully hide their intentions; social deception rolls suffer -1d.
  • When they attempt betrayal or flee, their shadow lags or points the wrong way (GM adds a tell or complication).

Push It: Bind the shadow to a Shadow's Debt [4] timer. When it fills (through lies, harm, cowardice), the shadow manifests as an echo-spirit that can testify or act once on your whispered command.

Curse of the Unanswered Reflection

CURSEHigh10 XP

Type: Long; Far   Materials: A mirror cracked in three pieces, each named for a truth the target refuses.

Effect: Mark a Refusal [6] timer on the target (individual or faction). Each time they double down on denial, cruelty, or willful ignorance, advance the timer.

  • At 3/6: distorted reflections whisper failings (GM adds fear, doubt, paranoia as Conditions).
  • At 6/6: a Reckoning Scene is forced — confrontation, trial, or nightmare where the hidden truth is dragged into the open.

Price: Identity (mandatory). Mark +1 Identity Strain.

Suppression and Witness

Witchcraft hates clarity. When a working is cast under scrutiny, doctrine, lanternlight, or formal correction, increase DV by 1. On a miss (or on a complication), the GM may generate SB toward reprisal, exposure, or a hunter's response.

Witch Orders and Their Rites

Witchcraft is not unified. Each Order encodes doctrine, taboo, and price into its rites, shaping how power is expressed and what it consumes in return. To join an Order is to accept its view of what must be sacrificed for magic to work.

The Night Court of Ilvane

The Night Court teaches that beauty is leverage, promises are weapons, and truth is negotiable under moonlight. Ilvane's witches defend ambiguity and punish certainty.

Doctrine: Reality bends most easily where desire and fear overlap. An oath half-kept is more powerful than one fulfilled.

Observances

  • Never speak an unvarnished truth beneath the open moon.
  • All bargains must contain a flaw, spoken or hidden.
  • Broken promises are never wasted.

Signature Rites

Thorn of Oaths-Broken

HEXStandard

Impose a spiritual wound on a target who has broken a vow.

  • The target suffers Oath-Bleed: Position is reduced by 1 when acting against their word.
  • On a miss, the witch inherits the recoil of the broken oath.

Price: Shame or Shadow.

Moon-Veil Compact

VEILLow

Wrap a subject in fae ambiguity.

  • Gain +1d to deception or concealment for the scene.
  • Leaves a glamour-tell visible only in reflections.

Price: Shadow.

The Ash Hags of Morag

Morag's witches embody rot, endings, and consequence. They do not forgive; they conclude.

Doctrine: Everything ends. Magic exists to make sure it ends properly.

Observances

  • Never reverse a punishment once begun.
  • Bones are records; treat them with respect.
  • Mercy is a lie told by those who fear endings.

Signature Rites

Bone-Truth Casting

REVEALStandard

Read consequence in bone or ash.

  • Ask one question about an unavoidable outcome.
  • The answer cannot be softened.

Price: Shame.

Curse of Settled Ash

  • Create a Decay [6] timer that advances when the target resists accountability.
  • At completion, a final reckoning scene occurs.

Price: Identity (mandatory).

The Handmaidens of Livaea

Livaea's witches rule through attraction, influence, and emotional architecture. Power flows through wanting.

Doctrine: No one is free of desire. Control what is desired, and you control the world.

Observances

  • Never refuse a sincere confession.
  • Desire must always be mirrored, never denied.
  • Public humiliation is forbidden; private leverage is sacred.

Signature Rites

Price: Shame.

Price: Shadow.

The Rainmaidens of Raéyn

Raéyn's witches are tempestuous elementalists whose emotions shape storm and tide.

Doctrine: Emotion is not weakness. It is weather.

Observances

  • Never deny your mood.
  • Water must be returned to water.
  • Storms demand witnesses.

Signature Rites

Tidebreaker Surge

STORMStandard

Redirect momentum in a conflict.

  • Allies gain +1 Position; enemies suffer -1d for one exchange.

Price: Shadow.

Mist-Crowned Omen

  • Ask one question about imminent danger or opportunity.

Price: Shame.

The Thorns of Malachai

The Thorns are not servants of a patron. They are adherents to a principle: identity is harvestable. What is discarded can be claimed, reshaped, and weaponized.

Doctrine: What the world refuses to hold must be taken. Forgotten selves are resources.

Observances

  • Never destroy an identity fragment while it still remembers pain.
  • Names are extracted, not spoken.
  • Witnesses are liabilities.

Signature Rites

  • Remove or distort a memory, bond, or role for the scene or longer.

On Familiars

A familiar is not a pet, a tool, or a summoned thing. A familiar is a shared consequence. It forms when a witch externalizes part of their shadow, memory, instinct, or unspoken need into a living conduit that can act, witness, and answer back. Once bound, neither witch nor familiar remains whole in the way they were before.

A Familiar is a persistent bond that allows a witch to relocate risk, perception, and cost. It amplifies magic by distributing consequence — but never removes it.

Creating a Familiar

To create a familiar, a witch performs a binding at a meaningful threshold and commits to an ongoing obligation.

Requirements

  • A willing or capturable vessel (animal, spirit, echo, object-entity).
  • A threshold significant to the witch.
  • A personal offering that cannot be reclaimed.

The Binding

State:

  • What the Familiar Knows (instinct, secret, fear, hunger).
  • What the Familiar Wants (safety, sensation, purpose, attention).
  • What the Witch Gave Up (memory, role, bond, certainty).

Roll Spirit + Witchcraft vs. DV 4.

  • Success: The bond forms cleanly.
  • Partial: The bond forms with a flaw (GM defines a behavioral complication).
  • Miss: The bond forms — but the familiar defines the terms of attention, need, or return.

Mark +1 Identity Strain. This mark cannot be avoided.

Familiar Nature

Choose or define the familiar's nature. This shapes how it interacts with magic and consequence.

NatureExpression
AnimalHeightened senses, instinctual reactions, physical presence.
SpiritInsubstantial movement, omen-bearing, threshold traversal.
EchoMemory replay, voice mimicry, emotional residue.
ConstructInscription, containment, durability, symbolic action.
ParasiteHunger-driven aid, escalation under stress.

Bond Track

Each familiar has a Bond Track [4] shared with its witch.

Mark Bond when:

  • The familiar assists in a working.
  • The familiar witnesses violence, betrayal, or confession.
  • The witch Pushes a working through the familiar.

When the Bond Track fills, choose one:

  • The familiar demands fulfillment (food, secrecy, rest, release).
  • The familiar acts independently in line with its nature.
  • The bond destabilizes; the GM introduces a rupture or misalignment.

Clear Bond only by honoring the familiar's needs.

Using a Familiar

A familiar may be invoked once per scene without a roll for minor assistance consistent with its nature. For greater effect, choose one option and roll Attribute + Witchcraft:

Extend Perception

See, hear, smell, or sense through the familiar.

  • Gain +1d on awareness or tracking.
  • Cost: Mark Bond.

Relay a Working

Cast a working as if you were present where the familiar is.

  • Increase DV by 1.
  • Cost: Familiar shares all backlash.

Absorb Consequence

Redirect fallout from a working.

  • Convert 1 Shadow or Shame into 1 Bond.
  • On a miss, the familiar manifests the consequence physically or socially.

Familiars and Cost

Familiars do not erase price — they translate it.

Shadow Through the Familiar

Shadow may appear as:

  • Unnatural behavior.
  • Aggression or withdrawal.
  • Physical mutation or spectral distortion.

Shame Through the Familiar

Shame may cause:

  • Avoidance of certain people or places.
  • Vocalizations or gestures at inopportune times.
  • Involuntary displays of guilt or fear.

Identity Through the Familiar

Identity loss may:

  • Bind the familiar more tightly than intended.
  • Transfer a trait, memory, or name-fragment.
  • Cause the familiar to speak truths the witch cannot.

Harm to Familiars

Familiars cannot be safely replaced.

When a familiar is harmed:

  • The witch immediately marks +1 Identity Strain.
  • The witch rolls Spirit + Endurance vs. DV 3.

On failure, gain a lingering Condition tied to the harm.

If a familiar is destroyed:

  • The witch permanently loses something bound during creation.
  • All Bond clears.
  • The witch gains a recurring omen, scar, or echo.

Observances of Familiar-Binding

Common Observances

  • Never force a familiar to cross a threshold you would refuse yourself.
  • Do not lie to your familiar; silence is acceptable, deception is not.
  • Feed the familiar before feeding yourself after a working.
  • Do not bind more than one familiar without release or death between.

Signs of a Failing Bond

  • The familiar avoids eye contact or reflection.
  • Shared dreams fracture or repeat.
  • The familiar reacts before the witch notices danger.
  • Names spoken near the familiar echo incorrectly.

Witchcraft Followers

Witchcraft followers are not merely assistants — they are living conduits of power, silent witnesses to hidden truths, and keepers of sacred obligations. A Witchcraft follower exists at the threshold between the mundane and the magical, bound by pacts, oaths, and the ever-present weight of consequence.

The Nature of Witchcraft Followers

Witchcraft followers have willingly or unwillingly bound themselves to the forces of the Witchcraft system. Unlike ordinary allies, they exist where the world's hidden currents flow most strongly — extensions of the witch's pact, bearing the weight of Obligation, the touch of Shadow, and the ever-present threat of the Unanswered Reflection.

Follower Types in Witchcraft

The Oathsworn

Born from the moment a follower swears a sacred promise to a Patron, the Oathsworn are the most common form of Witchcraft follower.

Characteristics

  • Carries a visible mark of their oath (a sigil, scar, or token).
  • Can sense when the oath is in peril.
  • May speak the true name of the Patron when called upon.

Magical Condition States

True-BoundOath is strong; +1 die to actions related to the Patron's domain.
FracturedOath is strained; -1 die to Patron-related actions; +1 Obligation segment.
ShatteredOath is broken; the follower becomes a Wraith, seeking to make the witch pay.

The Threshold-Keepers

These followers walk the edge between worlds, maintaining the boundaries that keep the witch's power contained.

Characteristics

  • Can sense and reinforce magical boundaries.
  • May temporarily lower a Ward for the witch.
  • Drawn to crossroads, doorways, and places of transition.

Magical Condition States

Ward-SafeThreshold connection strong; +1 die to boundary-related actions.
FrayedThreshold connection weakening; -1 die to boundary actions; +1 Exposure on threshold use.
BreachedThreshold connection broken; may attract unwanted attention from beyond.

The Shadow-Speaking

Bound to the very essence of Shadow, these followers perceive the unspoken truths that haunt the witch's path.

Characteristics

  • Sees the shadows that follow the witch.
  • Can speak the words that should remain unspoken.
  • Haunted by the things they've witnessed.

Magical Condition States

Clear-SightedShadow-sight is strong; +1 die to insight/truth-detection.
CloudedShadow-sight is weakening; -1 die to insight/truth-detection; +1 Exposure on truth-seeing.
ShroudedShadow-sight is lost; may see false shadows or be consumed by darkness.

Universal Witchcraft Follower Mechanics

Obligation Tracking

Witchcraft followers have their own Obligation timer, tied to the witch's pact.

  • Obligation Timer: 6 segments (tied to the witch's Obligation Capacity).
  • Filling the Timer: At 3 segments, the follower gains +1 die to actions related to the pact. At 6 segments, a demand from the Patron is made of the witch through the follower.
  • Clearing Obligation: The witch must perform a service for the Patron to reduce the follower's Obligation timer.

Shadow Exposure

Witchcraft followers accumulate Shadow Exposure from proximity to the witch's power.

  • 1: Can see the witch's Shadow as a faint aura.
  • 2: Hears the whispers of the Unanswered Reflection.
  • 3 (Overexposed): Suffers Fatigue 1, -1 die to all rolls, and may trigger a minor Shadow event.

Shadow Harm

Magical followers track Shadow Harm, representing damage to their connection with the shadow world.

  • 1: The shadow world begins to bleed into their reality.
  • 2: Suffers -1 die to social rolls with those who cannot see the Shadow.
  • 3 (Dissonant): Cannot use their magical abilities; may attract Shadow entities.

Magical Assistance

Witchcraft followers can assist in witchcraft-related actions:

  • Standard Assistance (1 Boon): Grant +1 die to witchcraft rolls within their specialty; share their insight about the ritual or Patrons.
  • Ritual Assistance (2 Boon): Let the witch borrow their connection for one roll; stabilize a ritual about to go awry; reveal a hidden truth about the ritual's requirements.

How to Gain a Witchcraft Follower

Witchcraft followers are not easily acquired. They require:

  • A significant story moment that creates the pact or bond.
  • A clear oath or commitment between the witch and the follower.
  • A narrative reason for the follower to have their connection to the Witchcraft system.

Gaining a Witchcraft follower costs 4 XP, but comes with an immediate 2 Obligation segments that the witch must bear.

The Cost of Connection

Witchcraft followers are not mere tools — they carry the weight of the witch's choices. When a follower falls, it is not just a loss of an ally; it is a fracture in the pact itself.

  • When a follower dies while in service, the witch gains 3 Obligation segments.
  • The witch's next ritual has a -1 die penalty (the weight of loss).
  • The follower's spirit may return as a Shadow entity, seeking resolution.

Example: The Oathsworn Follower

Name: Anya the Steadfast
Patron: The Gallow's Bell
Oath Mark: A small bell-shaped scar on her left palm
Starting Condition: True-Bound

Oathsworn Condition State Table

True-Bound+1 die to actions related to the Gallow's Bell's domain (truth, justice).
Fractured-1 die to Patron-related actions; +1 Obligation segment.

  • Oath-Call: Once per scene, Anya can call on the Gallow's Bell to verify a statement; the speaker must speak truth or suffer 1 Harm.
  • Ward-Safe: Once per ritual, she can stabilize the ritual space, reducing the risk of Backlash by one level.

Oathsworn Shadow Exposure Effects

  • 1: Sees the faint glow of truth in the air.
  • 2: Hears the echoes of unspoken promises.
  • 3 (Overexposed): Suffers Fatigue 1, -1 die to all rolls, and may see the shadows of broken oaths.

Witchcraft Follower Creation

To create a Witchcraft follower:

  1. Choose a follower type (Oathsworn, Threshold-Keeper, or Shadow-Speaking).
  2. Determine their connection to the witch (how the pact was formed).
  3. Give them a distinctive mark or trait related to their connection.
  4. Set their starting condition state (usually True-Bound or Clear-Sighted).
  5. Determine one special action they can perform related to their connection.

The Price of Power

Witchcraft followers are not meant to be permanent. They embody the witch's choices, and like all things in the world of Witchcraft, they carry the weight of consequence. When a follower breaks, it is not just a loss — it is a story. When they thrive, they prove that even in a world of shadows, there can be light.

The most powerful witchcraft lies not in the spells cast or the pacts made, but in the bonds forged and the promises kept. It is in these bonds that the true power of the Witchcraft system reveals itself — not as domination, but as the sacred responsibility of connection in a world that would rather forget.

The Unanswered Reflection

When a follower dies while in service to a witch, the Unanswered Reflection intensifies. The witch must:

  • Mark 3 Obligation segments immediately.
  • Suffer -1 die to all rolls until they perform an act of penance for the fallen follower.
  • Face the Shadow of the follower, who will appear at inopportune moments to remind them of their failure.

The Shadow of the follower is not an enemy, but a manifestation of the Unanswered Reflection — the weight of a bond broken too soon. It does not attack, but it is the reminder that every choice has consequences, and every bond carries a price.

The Living Pact

Witchcraft followers embody the pact between witch and Patron. They are not tools to be used, but companions to be honored. When a witch respects this bond, the power of the pact grows stronger. When they take it for granted, the shadows deepen.

The true measure of a witch is not in the spells they cast, but in the bonds they keep. In the world of Witchcraft, every promise is a thread, and the world itself is woven from the threads of unbroken oaths.

"The follower is not your tool, but your mirror. In them, you see the weight of your choices. In their eyes, you see the cost of your power."
— The Gallow's Bell, as recorded in the Book of the Unbroken Oath
