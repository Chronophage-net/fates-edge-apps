What This Is.

A maritime campaign framework for Fate's Edge: naval heists, blockade running, exploration, and faction warfare across the Amaranthine Sea under the Kahfagian "bottle." Built to use Position/Effect, Story Beats (SB), and Timers. Tuned to Oshiira's limited passage, Ykrul pressure from the west, Ashaan corsairs through the reed-chains, and the politics of Free Havens, Vilikari smugglers, and Linns dock unions.

Safety & Sensitivity.

This theater includes press-gangs, seizure courts, and Ashaani slaving content, which can be intense. Use lines/veils, X-card-style tools, and Session Zero to set expectations; reframe slavers as "press-gangers/abductors" if your table prefers. Represent every people as multifaceted — priests, poets, pilots — not caricatures.

1. The Setting

The Amaranthine Sea and Titan's Throat

The Amaranthine Sea is a semi-enclosed inland ocean of busy trade lanes, sudden fogs, and spirit-haunted shoals. Its primary western mouth is throttled by a labyrinth of islands, reefs, and boom-chains called Titan's Throat: a natural choke that the Kahfagian Admiralty has engineered into an artificial bottle.

Key Theaters (Node Map)

  • Western Reach — long swell, fast water; Ykrul longboats probe here.
  • Reef Cuts — Vilikari eel-channels; only low-draft pilots dare them.
  • Amber Straits — spirit buoys, pilot tests, and rangefinders.
  • Bottle Chain — lantern posts and boom-gates maintained by Kahfagia.
  • Saffron Lane — Oshiiran convoy corridor with timed windows and lamp codes.
  • Harbor Underbelly — dockside warrens; Linns stevedores control lists and rumor.
  • Dolmis Narrows — treacherous eastern strait: city-league pilots, tolls, courts.
  • Eastern Reach (Chain of Reeds) — Ashaan delta approaches; reed-winds and hidden inlets.
  • Free Havens (e.g., Sable Key, Indigo Shoals) — neutral anchorages and gray courts.

Titan's Throat at a Glance

  • Boom-Anchors: chained logs, iron links, and embedded purity bells.
  • Lantern Pylons: weekly phrases; failure ticks Papers Scrutiny.
  • Spirit Buoys: weigh "soul-scent" for contraband, blood-debt, or bondage.
  • Pilot Rights: only registered pilots may shift Position safely in the channels.

The Blockade System

How the Bottle Works

  1. Layered Detection: lookouts, lamp phrases, spirit buoys, and keel drags.
  2. Timed Windows: Oshiiran convoys receive fixed slots; all other traffic delays.
  3. Mobile Courts: shipboard magistrates seize, fine, or brand papers.
  4. Public Duels: officers may demand "courtesy duels" to settle suspicion without bloodshed — win Position, not necessarily lives.

2. Core Mechanics

This chapter merges fast, table-facing procedures with optional depth. Use Fate's Edge Position/Effect, Story Beats (SB), and Timers. Ship TAGS justify advantage, tools, or Effect.

Naval Movement and Positioning

Two Scales of Play

  • Theater Scale (Node Map). Move between zones (Western Reach, Reef Cuts, Amber Straits, etc.). Each move is a leg resolved by one Pilot/Navigator test; hazards may tick timers.
  • Tactical Scale (Range Bands). When exact distance matters for guns, rites, or boarding, switch to Range Bands.

Range Bands at Sea

BandDescription and Implications
CloseBoarding distance; grapples, cutlass work, harpoons, powder & fire pots. Boarding occurs at Close and becomes its own scene.
NearSame vessel cluster; shouting distance, coordinated maneuvers, ballista/harpoon range.
FarVisual contact; signal flags/lantern codes, long-range ballista or storm-calling.
AbsentBeyond horizon; requires a Travel Timer [4] to close, or a kite/lantern relay, spirit messenger, or courier.

Naval Movement Actions (Tactical)

  • Sail. Shift one Range Band on success; Dash attempts a second shift at −1 Position if it fails.
  • Maneuver. Complex positioning (flank, cut-off, evade) uses Pilot + Navigation vs. DV 2–4; success may also grant +1 Effect or impose −1 Position on a foe.
  • Drift. Dominant drift masks wake/noise using Rigger + Lore; on failure a nearby picket hears you, or Patrol Net +1.
  • Anchor / Drop Sail. Secure the ship under pressure with a Crew Muster test (Presence + Command); failure scatters crew or marks Rigging Damage.

Theater Movement (per Leg)

  1. State Speed Profile: Stealth Drift (−1 leg; +Position), Standard Run (normal), Press the Sails (+1 leg; −Position on a failure).
  2. Pilot Test: Wits + Navigation (+1 die for relevant leverage: pilot-token, eel-chart, convoy slot).
  3. Set Start Position by Successes: 0–1 Desperate, 2–3 Controlled, 4+ Dominant. (A 10 counts as 2 successes.)
  4. Hazards: If a leg lists hazards (boom, fog, reef), resolve or tick a timer (GM).

Chases on the Node Map

Track Quarry Lead [6] and Pursuer Closure [6]; opposed Pilot tests at each leg tick the winner's timer +1. Catch at 6 ⇒ shift to Tactical at Near. Escape at 6 ⇒ chase ends.

Position at Sea

  • Dominant — clear weather, pilots, known water; errors usually tick paperwork, not blood.
  • Controlled — contested water, moderate weather, eyes upon you; errors cost time or minor harm.
  • Desperate — storms, shoals, superior force; the acting ship re-rolls one success on that action.

Ship Combat

Conflict Modes

  • Side-Based Turns (Recommended). Each side chooses up to two distinct ship actions per round (different roles): Helm (change Range/maneuver), Rig (repairs, grapples, smoke), Guns (ballista/harpoon/chain-shot), Board (grapple/cross/secure), Ritual (weather/ward/veil).
  • Initiative Variant. Roll Wits + Command; highest acts first. Each PC/NPC ship role takes one action per round.

Combat Procedure (Both Modes)

  1. Declare Engagement. GM sets Position from fiction (weather, surprise, Range).
  2. Assign Roles. Pilot, Rigger/Engineer, Gunner, Boarder/Cutter, Whisper/Ritualist, Signal/Face.
  3. Take Actions. Resolve rolls; apply Range/Position; spend/earn SB.
  4. Resolve Consequences. Mark damage timers, shift Position, tick scene timers.

Boarding Sequence (Three Beats)

  1. Grapple (Rig/Gunner): Craft or Gunnery at Close. Success ⇒ proceed.
  2. Cross (Cutter): Athletics or Melee; BOARDING NETS +1 die; fail ⇒ mark Crew Morale or Rigging.
  3. Secure (All): clear deck leaders, cut lines if needed; success shifts to personal combat or a surrender test.

Signals, Ramming, and Command

  • Signal. Presence + Sway/Etiquette to coordinate allies or demoralize foes; on success, grant +1 die or impose −1 die for a round.
  • Ram (Desperate). Body + Athletics/Pilot vs. target Hull; on success mark both ships' Hull; great Effect may also Grapple.
  • Command Presence. Presence + Command to steady crew, cancel a panic, or seize initiative for one role.

Ship Condition: Quick or Detailed

Quick Play: Ship Integrity [6] — when full, the ship is Unseaworthy; further marks risk sinking or forced beaching.

Detailed Tracks (replace Integrity):

  • Hull Damage [4] — breaches/leaks; on fill: Sinking.
  • Rigging Damage [4] — on fill: no Range changes.
  • Crew Morale [4] — on fill: panic/surrender test.
  • Fire/Explosion [4] — on fill: catastrophic loss unless contained.

Hull Integrity Test: Body + Lore to resist catastrophic damage. Crew Loyalty Test: Presence + Command to maintain order.

Magic at Sea (Combat Note)

  • Unstable Platform. Casting at Controlled: −1 die; at Desperate: −2 dice unless anchored/lashed.
  • Backlash Amplification. Rites that misfire tick Ship Integrity (or Fire/Morale) in addition to personal harm.
  • Patron Interference. Naval battles increase omen/demand frequency at GM discretion.
  • Ritual Constraints. Major rites require a stabilized anchor point and mark Obligation.

Inspection and Paperwork Systems

Checkpoint Scene Procedure

  1. Declare Cover. What papers/story are you presenting?
  2. Set Position. Etiquette, bribes, or favors can improve Position.
  3. Primary Test. Presence + Etiquette/Deception, or Wits + Lore/Craft for paperwork.
  4. Secondary Checks. Keel drag, muster, sigil scan; each failure ticks Papers Scrutiny.
  5. Outcomes. Dominant: pass and reduce Blockade Awareness. Controlled: pass with a condition (escort, stamp, toll). Desperate: board/search, duel, or seizure court.

The Papers Game

Oshiira Transit Pack (Legitimate): Saffron Tab (convoy slot), Amber Bill (cargo), Lantern Phrase (weekly code), Pilot's Knot (navigator sigil). Missing/mismatched elements tick Papers Scrutiny +1.

Forgery Challenges

  • Downtime (Prepared). Perfect match: Craft + Lore, DV 3, proper tools.
  • On Approach (Time Pressure). Improvised: DV 4.
  • Live Boarding (Desperate). DV 5+; on failure, expect seizure or a duel.

Bribes, Duels, and Cutouts

  • Bribe Track [3]. Each paid step grants +1 Position or cancels a tick; on fill you're on a list (future Audit entanglement).
  • Courtesy Duel. One PC vs. an officer (Duel/Etiquette/Melee); win ⇒ safe conduct and −1 Awareness.
  • Cutouts. Dock fixers (Linns/Vilikari) substitute their rep once per score: consume a favor to avoid a full inspection.

Faction Standing and Reputation

Faction Standing Timers [6]

Each major faction keeps a Standing timer with your crew.

  • −6 to −5 (Hostile). Bounties, blacklists, kill-on-sight, or seizure.
  • −4 to −3 (Unfavorable). More inspections, higher tolls, patrol "attention."
  • −2 to 2 (Neutral). Standard treatment, normal prices, limited help.
  • 3 to 4 (Favorable). Easier inspections, better prices, occasional favors.
  • 5 to 6 (Favored). Convoy slots, legal shelter, special services (pilot tokens, codes).

Changing Standing

  • Favors/Jobs. +1–2 for completing marked work; −1–2 for insults or unpaid debts.
  • Protocols. Following maritime etiquette can improve Position and Standing over time.
  • Court Results. Win a Free Haven case: +1 with courts. Humiliate an admiralty: −1 with Kahfagia.

4. Magic at Sea: Raéyn's Wake & the Kraken's Chain

The Amaranthine is a choir of winds and a vault of pressure. Above, Raéyn the Storm Queen writes with lightning and breath; below, Khemesh, the Kraken of the Deep, constricts with current, chain, and weight. Between them, other Patrons make claims — veils, truths, bargains, and songs. This chapter frames sea-magic through their contest and gives procedures for rites, summoning, backlash, and the Cantor's work aboard.

Design Note.

These rules expand Magical Combat Limitations, Weather Rituals, and Patron Integration at Sea. Use Devotion/Obligation, Position/Effect, and Timers as the backbone. Where fiction disagrees with tables, fiction rules — name what it looks like on your deck.

Cosmology of the Waterline

Raéyn the Storm Queen (Breath, Surge, Mercy)

Raéyn governs winds, squalls, and the breath that lifts sails. She favors movement that spares the helpless and courage that holds formation. Her omens taste of ozone; her touch speeds rigging and clears lungs. Raéyn abhors stagnant waters used for cruelty.

Khemesh the Kraken (Constriction, Pressure, Debt)

Khemesh claims the column from keel to trench. He binds with ropes, chains, oaths, and barnacled ledgers. Calm seas that smother motion and currents that drag below are his tools. He loves proper weights, tolls, and the price of disobedience.

Conducting Magic Aboard

The Ship as Circle

A seaworthy hull is a moving ritual diagram. Treat Mast, Bells, Lanterns, Keel, and Figurehead as anchor points. A rite needs three of five to be stable. Fewer anchors: +1 DV and counts as Controlled. No anchors: Desperate and primed for backlash.

Casting Windows & Position

  • Dominant. Steady way on, trimmed canvas, crew at stations. Standard DV.
  • Controlled. Maneuvering hard, cross chop, or contested water. −1 die or +1 DV (choose).
  • Desperate. Storm-on-bow, reefs, boarding melee. −2 dice or +2 DV; on backlash, extend to crew (tick Ship Integrity or Morale).

The Barometer Track [6]

Place a visible track between Breath (Raéyn) on the left and Pressure (Khemesh) on the right. At scene start, mark 1–2 toward whichever force dominates (weather, depth, fiction).

  • Breath 1–2: Wind rites −1 DV; constricting effects +1 DV.
  • Breath 3–4: Free Wind Call once; chain/grapple actions are Dominant.
  • Pressure 1–2: Binding, ballast, and holdfast rites −1 DV; sailing Maneuvers +1 DV.
  • Pressure 3–4: Free Holdfast once; capsize checks +1 tier of consequence.

Shift the marker when omens land, when Cantors sing properly (toward Breath), or when debts are enforced (toward Pressure).

Naval Travel and Random Encounters

Travel Procedure (Per Sea Leg)

  1. Plot the Course. Choose a route across sea zones; each leg is a Travel Timer [4] (short) or [6] (long).
  2. Assign Roles. Each PC (or PO/NPC) claims a travel role: Guide, Scout, Quartermaster, Watch.
  3. Advance the Leg. Each watch, resolve one key action (Guide, Scout, etc.). On a success, mark the Travel Timer +1; on a critical, +2; on a failure, +0 and the GM spends SB or ticks a hazard timer.

Collapse: Obligation hits 6 and a Doctrine is broken on screen: Patron withdraws; convert to Break the Titan (secular reform) or Pirate Kings (fallen zeal).

Endgame Set Pieces: The Procession of Lamps (fleet-wide ritual under storm-lit skies); Court of Salt (adjudicate a maritime law before five factions; the verdict shapes the map).

10. Adventure Outlines

Twelve drop-in mini-adventures and two showpiece arcs for the Amaranthine. Each outline includes a Hook, Objective, Key Faces, scene Procedure & Timers, Complications, Rewards, and Scaling. Use the Heist Engine, Ship & Crew rules, and Faction Standing throughout.

Content Note. This theater touches on blockade violence and Ashaani coercive labor practices. Set Lines/Veils at Session Zero, use safety tools, and reframe elements (e.g., "bondage detection buoys" as contract-violation alarms) to fit your table.

Mini-Adventures (Drop-Ins)

1. The Scent of Fear.

Hook. Contract-buoys report unlawful bondage aboard several "clean" hulls queued for Oshiiran transit. Objective. Trace the false positives without tipping off the saboteurs.

Key Faces: Scribe-Pilot Orel Vass (by-the-lantern), Free Haven broker Sola Nemet, covert Kahfagian runner "The Quiet Bosun." Timers: Audit Chain [6] (dock, manifest, buoy), Saboteur Window [4]. Complications (d4): 1) buoy key swapped in court; 2) a rival crew frames the PCs; 3) a witness vanishes at sea; 4) the "victims" are embedded agents pinging the net. Rewards: −1 Papers Scrutiny for one lantern cycle; Standing +1 with Free Havens. Scaling: Tier I: one pier. Tier II–III: three synchronized docks across two ports.

2. Pilot's Gambit.

Hook. Dolmis pilots strike; the harbor sits idle. Objective. Move a priority hull through the Narrows without official pilotage.

Faces: Pilot-Mistress Dara Choksun (strike leader), "false pilot" Grin Halvek (Vilikari), Dock Judge Ibn Kas. Timers: Reef Cut [6], Strike Violence [4], Smuggler Lift [4]. Complications: surprise counter-current; pilot boat pursuit; counterfeit charts. Rewards: friendly Pilot List contact; a hidden cut (treat one leg as Far to Absent). Scaling: add night fog (Dominant) or a hunter cutter shadowing.

3. Reef Runners.

Hook. The Vilikari syndicate sells access to eel-cuts. Objective. Run the secret channels, then sever the syndicate's leverage.

Faces: Reef-Mother Veika (tide-reader), Old Iron (syndicate oarmaster), the Many-Eyed Idol (temple ward). Timers: Reef Maze [6], Ward Attention [4], Syndicate Payback [6]. Complications: living coral grinds the hull; the idol demands an oath; Linns reef-fishers claim taboo. Rewards: a cut map — once per arc, shift one sea pursuit by +1 Range. Scaling: temple allies shorten the maze; angering the idol ticks Ward Attention +2.

4. Lantern Lies.

Hook. Last cycle's lantern phrases still open one tower. Objective. Find the leak before a ghost convoy sails through.

Faces: Lantern-Keeper Eshra Tan, tower acolyte "Polliwog," Free Haven "historian" Master Gray. Timers: Leak Source [6], Ghost Convoy [4], Hunter Ready [4]. Complications: the leak is ritual, not ink; an ancestral fleet repeats old codes. Rewards: a Ghost Convoy tactic once (treat detection as false). Scaling: Tier III: three towers sync; a miscode cascade heist.

5. Ballista Diplomacy.

Hook. Ykrul longboats demand tribute along a headland. Objective. Resolve the standoff without igniting a clan war.

Faces: Skain of the Three Oars (Ykrul chief), Linns wreck-diver Tarin Rope, Wreck-Spirit Salt-Red. Timers: Clan Honor [6], Wreck Claim [4], Hot Blood [4]. Complications: tribute is funeral duty; the wreck shelters sacred bones. Rewards: a Ykrul oath-token — +1 die to parley with longboats. Scaling: add a rival captain trying to snipe the claim.

6. The Silent Fleet.

Hook. Ships vanish under fair winds. Objective. Track, enter, and survive an ancient collector's lair.

Faces: The Archivist Below (leviathan/construct), survivor Meryn Fluke. Timers: Wake Trail [4], Abyss Door [6], Collection Protocol [6]. Complications: your hull gets cataloged; voices of prior crews tempt rest. Rewards: a "taken" hull returned; deep salvage rights. Scaling: add pressure wards; require AIRFEED kit and rites.

7. Paper Trail.

Hook. Forged Oshiira sets flood the market. Objective. Identify the master-plate and flip the press.

Faces: Forger Needle-Kei, customs hawk Captain Rhaz, guild scrivener Aunt Imra. Timers: Plate Hunt [6], Seizure Sweep [4], Guild Wrath [6]. Complications: the press is mobile; a court case hinges on your testimony. Rewards: −1 DV to Paper Shop in this port; Standing +1 (Administration). Scaling: Tier III: the plate is protected by oath-ink wards.

8. Storm Riders.

Hook. Raiders ambush only during perfect storms. Objective. Reveal the riders' purpose and choose a side.

Faces: Weather-mage Suri-of-the-Bays, coastal abbot Hako, militia commodore Fen Jarlan. Timers: Squall Window [4], Coastal Evac [6], Militia Fury [6]. Complications: the riders divert storms from villages, at traders' expense. Rewards: a Wind Call charm (once per arc, shift Weather to Favorable). Scaling: add a corsair convoy exploiting the storm lanes.

9. The Drowned City.

Hook. The tide reveals streets of a lost port. Objective. Explore, bargain, or flee its jealous keepers.

Faces: Tide-Warden Ashaelt, the Gong-Fishers (local kin), the Salt Judges (statues). Timers: Tide Window [4], Salt Law [6], Plunder Greed [4]. Complications: taking relics invokes trial by tide; the Judges speak true-names. Rewards: a tide charter (safe approach map) or a lawful relic. Scaling: make the approach Abyssal with living causeways.

10. Cargo Cult.

Hook. Island rites elevate cargo to covenant objects. Objective. Untangle ritual from racket without scorning local law.

Faces: Elder Pela (covenant keeper), fixer Black Tamas, novice Lumi. Timers: Rite of Welcome [4], Smuggler Spine [6], Offense Given [4]. Complications: rites mask bonded caches; insult brings a storm-ban. Rewards: a covenant safe-passage mark; a fence route to the inner market. Scaling: add a rival who weaponizes "sacrilege" against you.

11. The Ghost Net.

Hook. A Linns fishing fleet hits invisible walls. Objective. Free the lanes from spirit nets.

Faces: Net-Singer Iri, the drowned oath Chain-of-Names. Timers: Net Map [4], Appeasement Rite [6], Corsair Poach [4]. Complications: slavers exploit the snare; appeasement demands witness names. Rewards: +1 Standing with Linns; a Spirit Mask pattern. Scaling: the net is keyed to broken vows; requires atonement scenes.

12. Harbor Wars.

Hook. Two Free Havens slide toward open conflict. Objective. Cool the powder — or capitalize.

Faces: Harbor Masters Rav Kade and Sestrel Mora; third-party provocateur, Ashaan factor Nemed. Timers: Dock Riots [6], Provocation Trail [6], Seizure Court Trigger [4]. Complications: strike-breakers; tower letters leaked; a staged arson. Rewards: an Amnesty Night token; a bonded storage rate cut. Scaling: add a tower audit flotilla on day three.

Showpiece Adventures

A. The Titan's Heart

Scope. Multi-session blockade-break heist against Titan's Chain HQ. Premise. Expose the illegal seizure ledgers and miscode the chain for a night.

  1. Lantern Dust (Intel). Free Haven legwork: fill Ledger Map [6], Tower Schedules [4]. Faces: Lantern Auditor Peth, Signal-Nun Verri.
  2. Two Vectors (Insertion). Inside team rides a supply lighter; Outside team stages a cutter collision. Timers: Badge Scrutiny [6], Alarm Chain [6].
  3. The Pull (Extraction). Swap ledgers, plant false cycles. Consequence moves: Hunter Squadron Arrives [6], Lantern Dominion −2 if clean.
  4. The Run (Escape). Reef-cut chase under chain-shot; use Ghost Convoy or Spirit Mask. Final duel: Hunter-Captain Naima the Pale.

Victory. Publish ledgers and exit with one hull intact: Lantern Dominion −3, Standing +2 (Free Havens). Failure. Alarm cascade fills; your name is posted; the next arc starts at Hostile with Kahfagia. Set Pieces: tower lens duel (Seven Bell Court at sea), chain-cutter reef-surge, signal-book auction mid-fight.

B. Empire of the Deep

Scope. Underwater diplomacy and ruin run; 2–4 sessions. Premise. Seek the Deep Folk, earn a treaty, and open a sub-tide trade.

  1. The Girding. Acquire AIRFEED kit, rites, and a Leviathan Guide. Timers: Gear Fit [4], Surface Interference [4].
  2. Descent Through Ruins. Navigate the Rift Way [6]; hazards from the Diving chapter; a rival expedition nips your heels.
  3. The Court Below. Three petitions: Memory, Tribute, Restraint. Fail one → Court Suspicion [6] rises.
  4. Third Echo War. A hostile Deep faction strikes. Fight in 3D currents; protect the pact-stone.

Victory. Sign the Salt-and-Stone Accords: unlock a Sub-Tide Route (once per arc: bypass one checkpoint entirely). Failure. The sea closes; mark Sea's Disfavor [6] (weather swings harsher until atoned). Set Pieces: choir of bubbles parley, whale-mother escort, mirrored labyrinth of currents.

Linking Threads & Progression

Thread Seeds
