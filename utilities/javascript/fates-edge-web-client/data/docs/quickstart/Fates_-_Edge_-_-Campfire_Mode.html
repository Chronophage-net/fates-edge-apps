🏕️ Fate's Edge: Campfire Edition

✨ One Table, Any Playstyle – A Guide for Mixed‑Ability Groups

🎯 The Golden Rule

Everyone at this table uses the EXACT SAME dice roll.
Pick your Attribute + Skill. Roll that many d10s.
Count 6–9 as 1 success, 10 as 2 successes.
Compare to the Target Number (DV) set by the GM.

That's it. The dice always speak the same language.

📝 The Base Character (for everyone)

Fits on an index card. Distribute 1, 2, 2, 3 among the four attributes.

⚡ 1. Pick Your Stats

Body 💪 Being strong, tough, and athletic.
Wits 🧠 Being quick, sneaky, and perceptive.
Spirit ✨ Being brave, focused, and magical.
Presence 👑 Being charming, scary, and commanding.

🎯 2. Pick Your Skills (6 points, max 2 per skill)

Fight ⚔️ – sword, axe, spear, or punches
Move 🏃 – climb, sneak, hide, jump
Talk 🗣️ – persuade, lie, intimidate
Know 📚 – history, medicine, spot clues
Magic ✨ – weave spells, read runes
Craft 🔧 – pick locks, build traps, fix gear

❤️ Health Track (Stamina + Wounds)

  • Fatigue 😮‍💨 – you get tired. Track = your Body. Mark it when you do hard stuff.
  • Harm 🩸 – you get hurt. 1 = Sore, 2 = Badly Hurt, 3 = Down (can't fight).

🎲 The Resolution Loop

  1. Step 1: The GM sets the scene. “What do you do?”
  2. Step 2: Pick your Attribute + Skill.
  3. Step 3: The GM says the Difficulty (DV).
    • • DV 2 – Easy (kick open a rickety door)
    • • DV 3 – Normal (fight a guard)
    • • DV 4 – Hard (climb a cliff in rain)
    • • DV 5+ – Crazy (convince a king to surrender)
  4. Step 4: Roll the dice!
  5. Step 5: Look at the result (see table below).

🪙 The Two Treasuries

🪙 Player's Boons (luck coins)

  • ✓ Get them on Partial or Miss.
  • ✓ Hold up to 5.
  • ✓ Spend 1 to: re-roll a die, get +1 on your next roll, or activate a special item/pet.
  • (Veterans: convert 2 Boons → 1 XP at end of session!)

⚡ GM's Story Beats (trouble tokens)

  • ✓ Get them when players roll a 1.
  • ✓ Spend 1 – “A guard turns around.” (tiny)
  • ✓ Spend 2 – “Reinforcements arrive.” (big)
  • ✓ Spend 3 – “The floor collapses!” (huge)

🧩 Expansion Packs (add complexity later)

Players who want to keep it simple just use the Base Character above. Players who want more crunch can pick one of these packs.

📦 Pack 1: The Talented (simple feats)

  • Pick 3 cool things your character is great at. (e.g. “Duelist: +1 die when fighting one enemy”, “Thief: pick any normal lock without rolling”)
  • Mechanic: When you use a Stunt, you just say “I use my X” and roll with advantage (e.g. +1 die, or ignore a negative modifier).

📦 Pack 2: The Free Caster (easy magic)

  • You know 2 simple spells (e.g. Fire Bolt, Healing Touch).
  • Roll Wits + Magic. DV is usually 3 for a cool trick, 4 for a big blast.
  • Backlash: If you roll a 1, the GM does something annoying (your robe catches fire, you get pushed back). Improvise it!

📦 Pack 3: The Runekeeper (obligation)

  • You made a deal with a patron. You have 3 special moves (Rites).
  • To use a Rite: roll Spirit + Magic (DV 3).
  • Obligation: Mark a box (track = Spirit + Presence). If it fills, take 1 Fatigue. Clear it by doing a small favor for your patron.

📦 Pack 4: The Summoner (monster buddy)

  • You have a cool monster buddy (spirit). Summon it: spend 1 Boon (or take 1 Fatigue).
  • The buddy has 3 Health (Leash). It can do one simple thing per round (attack, scout, carry).
  • If it gets hit, it loses 1 Health. If it loses all Health, it vanishes and you take 1 Fatigue.
  • Veteran upgrade: track a full “Leash” track – but for kids, it’s just “pet with 3 HP.”

🧑‍🏫 GM Tips for Mixed‑Experience Tables

1. The Spotlight Shift
Rotate the spotlight. Let the simple Fighter roll a huge “Fight” check. Then, let the complex Summoner roll “Spirit + Magic”. They both roll the same dice, but the narrative looks different.

2. Color the Consequences
When the simple player rolls a 1, the problem is physical (“Your sword gets stuck”). When the complex player rolls a 1, the problem is mystical (“Your patron glares at you”).

3. The “Fiction First” Rule
If a player doesn’t understand their Rite, revert to the core rule. “What do you want to happen?” → “I want to make the enemy fall asleep.” → “Cool. Roll Spirit + Magic. DV 3. If you hit, they sleep. If you roll a 1, they get angry.”

4. Don’t Track Resources for Everyone
The kid tracks Boons + Fatigue. The veteran tracks Boons, Fatigue, Leash, and Obligation. That’s their job, not yours.

5. End of Session
Give everyone the same XP (e.g. 6 XP). The kid buys a new Stunt; the veteran buys a new Rite. They progress together.

📋 Quick Reference (for kids & everyone)

⚔️ Hit someone with a swordBody + Fight
👻 Sneak past a guardWits + Move
🧗 Climb a wallBody + Move
🗣️ Talk your way out of a fightPresence + Talk
✨ Cast a cool spellWits + Magic (or Spirit)
🔧 Fix a broken wheelWits + Craft
🔍 Notice a trapWits + Know
💪 Lift a heavy gateBody + (no skill, or Move)
🟩 6–9 = 1 Success 🟨 10 = 2 Successes 🟥 1 = GM gets a Trouble Token!

“The road remembers every traveler, no matter how they walk it.”

Start simple. If the kid wants to learn Summoner later, let them “upgrade” mid‑campaign.
If the veteran wants to play a basic Fighter to take a break, let them do that too.
