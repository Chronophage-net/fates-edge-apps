Character Backgrounds

Fate's Edge Player's Guide — Chapter 16

From the journal of a Thepyrgosi Witch Hunter:

They call it mercy when the Tower does it and cruelty when I do. The difference is a seal of wax and a hymn at the gate.

I was a bright spark once—could taste iron on my tongue when the air went thin before a working. The masters in Thepyrgos tested me, weighed me, and found me nearly enough. “Nearly” is a door that never opens again.

So I learned the other doors.

I carry writs that make people look away. I carry cord and a promise I do not speak aloud. The University pays for talent delivered in time; the city pays for fires that never start. Between those accounts, I balance what I can.

You will say I go too far. Sometimes I do. Sometimes the street is a tinderbox and a wrong whisper is a spark. When the bells of Thepyrgos answer the river wind, I walk the colonnade to the bursar's door and do not look at the windows. The work is ugly. The work is necessary. The night does not care which of those is true—only that the lamps keep burning.

Before your character took their first deliberate risk, they belonged somewhere. Backgrounds are the trail-dust on your boots, the school-ink on your fingers, the bell-pattern you still tap when frightened. They explain how you know what you know—and who still knows you. They are also the soil in which your Bonds and Complications take root.

Understanding Backgrounds

Backgrounds serve several important functions:

  • Provide narrative context for abilities and choices
  • Explain how you gained skills and connections
  • Create ties to regions and factions
  • Offer roleplaying hooks, responsibilities, and privileges
  • Seed reliable contacts, customs, and places that matter on-screen
  • Establish the foundation for Bonds and Complications that will shape your story

Mechanical Hooks

Choose one background at character creation. It grants:

  • Two Access Tags (narrative permissions; see below)
  • A Signature Contact (named NPC; Cap 1 follower, no XP cost; grants +1d once per scene when their help is plausible)
  • A Background Boon (once per session, gain +1d or reduce DV by 1 for a task strongly tied to the background)
  • An Obligation Timer [4] (when filled, a favor is called in, a duty recalled, or a price comes due—this is your starting Complication)

Access Tags

Tags describe where and with whom you have standing. Examples: Guild-Ledgered, Court-Announced, Veteran-of-the-Marches, Bell-Kin, Wind-Knot Tokens, Charter-Stamped. When a scene engages a tag, the GM may:

  • Waive a trivial gate (no roll)
  • Lower Position risk by one step for first contact
  • Offer a Devil's Bargain tied to that society's expectations

Weaving Bonds and Complications into Your Past

Your Background is not a static label. It is the first thread in a web of relationships and obligations that will define your character's story. As you build your Background, consider:

  • Who do you owe? Your Obligation Timer is not a punishment—it is a promise of future adventure. The person or institution you owe is already a part of your story.
  • Who owes you? Your Signature Contact is a beginning, not an end. They are someone who knows you, trusts you, or fears you. That relationship can deepen into a Bond over time.
  • What binds you to others? The best characters are defined not by what they have done, but by who they have done it with. Your Background's skills and tags suggest how you might form Bonds with other player characters.
“The road is long. Walk it with people you trust—or at least people you owe. Both will carry you.” — The Gray Wanderer

Background Benefits and Limits

Typical Benefits

  • Skill Access: Strong narrative justification for certain tests
  • Resource Access: Borrowed tools, rooms, shrines, or records (scene-time instead of XP)
  • Social Access: Entry to circles your tags recognize
  • Knowledge: Clues, maps, or customs that lower DV by 1
  • Reputation: Advantage on first impressions in-tag

Common Limitations

  • Duties: Responsibilities to house, guild, banner, or court
  • Restrictions: Codes, taboos, or public expectations
  • Enemies: Rival houses, crews, or schools
  • Secrets: Leverage others can pull
  • Dependencies: Loss of standing if you neglect ties

Creating a Custom Background

Build Procedure (Player & GM)

Pick 2 Access Tags, 1 Signature Contact, 1 Boon, and 1 Obligation Seed. Name places and people.

  • Origin: Where were you taught/raised/forged?
  • Training: Which two skills does this background naturally support?
  • Experiences: One memory that still changes how you act
  • Relationships: One ally, one rival; both want something
  • Values: What won't you let slide?
  • Complications: What unresolved obligation, feud, or secret follows you?

Mechanical Considerations

  • Boon Scope: DV −1 or +1d once/session when strongly on-theme
  • Contact Use: +1d as assist; cannot take independent actions unless purchased as a Follower (Cap2 XP)
  • Assets: Borrowing an institutional asset costs scene time (counts as upkeep for that asset this downtime) but 0 XP
  • Languages: Choose one tongue linked to the background at Conversational or better

The Obligation Timer

Each background includes an Obligation Timer [4]. It advances when:

  • You fail a roll tied to your background (GM discretion)
  • You accept a favor from your signature contact
  • The GM spends 1 SB to represent a duty recalled
  • You ignore a responsibility linked to your access tags

When the timer fills, something is due. The GM may choose or roll:

RollResult
1–2Minor favor, time, or tithe (1 Supply or a scene's work)
3Significant service, costly honesty, dangerous escort (a full scene)
4Defining ordeal: duel, audit, rite, or trial (potential Harm or permanent consequence)

Clear segments by honoring duties on-screen, sacrificing resources, or changing affiliation (which creates new timers).

Bonds and Complications in Play

Your Background is not just a list of skills and tags. It is the soil in which your character's relationships and obligations take root. The Bonds you form and the Complications you carry are the living threads of that soil.

Bonds as Narrative Levers

A Bond is a mutual relationship of trust, debt, or shared history between your character and another—whether a fellow player character, a trusted Follower, or a significant NPC who has earned a place in your story. Bonds are the connective tissue of Fate's Edge.

When you act on a Bond with intricate description, you gain 1 Boon (once per Bond per session). At Tier III (91–150 XP), Bonds between player characters also allow the transfer of up to 2 Boons as a free action once per scene.

  • Establish: Form a Bond with any PC, Follower, or significant NPC through shared experience, oath, or debt.
  • Activate: Once per session per Bond, when you act on it with intricate description, you gain 1 Boon.
  • Transfer (Tier III+): Once per scene, transfer up to 2 Boons to a Bonded PC as a free action.
  • Limit: You may have up to 3 active Bonds at a time (GM discretion for major story Bonds).
“The best Bonds are the ones that hurt to break. The road is long. Walk it with people you trust—or at least people who owe you. Both will carry you.” — The Gray Wanderer

The Obligation Timer as a Complication

Your Background's Obligation Timer is a Complication—a weight you carry forward into the story. It is not a punishment; it is a promise of future adventure. The person or institution you owe is already a part of your story.

When the timer fills, something is due. This can be:

  • A debt called in at the worst possible moment
  • A rival who has been waiting for you to return
  • A duty you cannot ignore without losing face
  • A secret that demands to be spoken

The GM may also spend Story Beats to advance your Obligation Timer when the fiction demands it, representing the pressure of your past catching up with you.

Mechanical Summary

Backgrounds, Bonds & Complications at a Glance

From Your Background

  • 2 Access Tags (narrative permissions)
  • 1 Signature Contact (Cap 1 follower, +1d assist once/scene)
  • 1 Background Boon (once/session, +1d or DV −1)
  • 1 Obligation Timer [4] (your starting Complication)
  • 1 language at Conversational or better

From Bonds

  • Form Bonds with PCs, Followers, or significant NPCs (max 3)
  • Once/session per Bond: act on it with intricate description → gain 1 Boon
  • Tier III+: transfer up to 2 Boons to a Bonded PC as a free action (once/scene)

From Complications

  • Your Obligation Timer is a Complication
  • When it fills, something is due (favor, duty, debt, secret)
  • The GM may spend SB to advance your timer
  • You may take additional Complications at character creation for +2 XP each (max +4 XP)

Background Combinations

Sequential

  • Veteran → City Watch: Access shifts from Muster Papers to Badge Rights
  • Scholar → Diplomat: Keep libraries, gain court

Parallel

  • Noble Merchant: Both Court-Announced and Guild-Ledgered; responsibilities multiply

Contrasting

  • Criminal Penitent: Underworld ties vs. temple vows; great hooks, sharp bargains

Using Backgrounds in Play

Player Applications

  • Name your places: the specific stall, shrine, or yard you know
  • Cash your Boon when you leverage that familiarity
  • Call on your contact to assist or to introduce a new lead
  • Use your Bonds to create shared stakes and mechanical advantage
  • Let your Obligation Timer guide your decisions—the price will come due eventually

GM Applications

  • Offer choices where background allegiance matters
  • Turn Access Tags into gates and keys
  • Spend Story Beats to complicate the old ties
  • Advance Obligation Timers when the fiction demands it
  • Use Backgrounds to seed Bonds between player characters

GM SB Menu: Background Complications

  • 1 SB: A rival claims your tag is invalid here; prove it or pay time
  • 2 SB: Your contact is busy or watched; help will cost you exposure

  • 4 SB: An enemy weaponizes your background—publicly

Background Quick Reference

At Creation

  • Choose: 2 Access Tags, 1 Signature Contact (Cap 1 assist), 1 Boon (once/session), 1 Obligation Seed.
  • Pick 1 language tied to the background at Conversational or better.
  • Bonds: You may form 1–2 Bonds with other player characters. Each Bond grants a once-per-session Boon.
  • Complications: You may take additional Complications at character creation for +2 XP each (max +4 XP).

In Play

  • Invoke tags to open doors or shift Position.
  • Call your contact for +1d assist (no independent actions).
  • Track the Obligation Timer [4]. Pay it on-screen.
  • Act on your Bonds to gain Boons and transfer resources.
  • Accept Complications as story fuel, not penalties.

Advancing

  • Upgrade contact to a Follower by paying XP (Cap2).
  • Add a second tag after a major arc or training.
  • Deepen a Bond into a Legacy Bond (see Chapter 17).
  • Resolve a Complication to clear an Obligation Timer or gain a new ally.
