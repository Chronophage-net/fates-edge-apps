Quick Start

Chapter 1 · Quick Start

What You Need to Play

Fate’s Edge is a game where you tell stories together. You need:

  • A Game Master (GM): The person who runs the world and plays everyone else.

  • Players: Each of you plays one main character.

  • Dice: A handful of ten-sided dice (d10s).

  • Character sheets: To track your hero.

  • This book: For reference.

The Core Loop in 60 Seconds

Every important action follows this simple loop:

  1. Declare what you do. Say what you want to achieve and how.

  2. The GM sets the stakes. They tell you the Difficulty (DV) and Position.

  3. Roll dice. Your dice pool is Attribute + Skill d10s.

  4. Count successes. Each 6+ is a success. Each 10 counts as 2 successes.

  5. The GM tells you what happens. Based on your result.

Your Character

Your character has:

  • Four Attributes: Body, Wits, Spirit, Presence (rated 1-5).

  • Twelve Core Skills: Things you’re trained in (rated 0-5).

  • Talents: Special abilities that make you unique.

Your dice pool is always Attribute + Skill.

The Dice

Roll your dice pool. Each die that shows:

Die Result What It Means
6-9 1 Success
10 2 Successes (critical!)
1 1 Story Beat (SB) for the GM
2-5 Nothing

Difficulty (DV)

The GM sets a target number you need to hit:

DV What It Means
2 Routine — almost guaranteed
3 Default — a real challenge
4 Hard — serious resistance
5 Extreme — a dramatic gamble

The standard ladder ends at DV 5. DV 6–8 is reserved for Tier IV+ epic-scale challenges; otherwise use Position, Effect, or a timer to raise the stakes.

Position

Position tells you how risky the action is:

Position How You Act Mechanical Effect
Dominant You have the advantage Re-roll one failure
Controlled Normal risk No re-rolls
Desperate You’re in serious danger Re-roll one success (never a 10)

The Outcome

Compare your successes to the DV:

Result Outcome What Happens
S \(\ge\) DV, SB=0 Clean Success You get what you want, no cost
S \(\ge\) DV, SB>0 Success with SB You get what you want, but the world pushes back
0 < S < DV Partial You make progress, but not all the way
S = 0 Miss You fail and the situation escalates; on a meaningful roll, gain 2 Boons

Boons and Story Beats

Boons (Your Resource)

  • Earn 1 Boon on a Partial. A meaningful Miss earns 2 Boons whether or not the roll generated Story Beats.

  • Spend Boons to: re-roll a die, improve your Position, or activate an Asset.

  • You can hold up to 5 Boons; trim to 2 at the end of a scene.

Story Beats (GM’s Resource)

  • Every time you roll a 1, the GM gains a Story Beat (SB).

  • The GM spends SB to add complications, escalate threats, or advance timers.

  • This keeps the story interesting — 1s are not punishments; they’re fuel for drama.

Quick Start Example

Lyra tries to pick a lock while guards patrol.

GM: “Controlled, DV 3. On a Miss, the guard hears you.”

Lyra rolls (Wits 3 + Craft 2 = 5 dice): 9, 6, 4, 2, 1

  • Successes: 9 and 6 = 2 successes vs DV 3

  • Result: Partial — the lock clicks, but Lyra gains 1 Boon

  • The 1 gives the GM 1 SB

GM: “The lock opens, but the last pin squeals. I spend the SB: the guard stops and turns his head. You have one beat to hide or act.”
